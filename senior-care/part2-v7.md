Pichle do saal mein Bharat ke har bade developer ne senior living me kuch na kuch move announce kiya hai. Lekin sab ek hi tareeke se nahi khel rahe. Companies ke do alag approaches hain.

## Approach 1: Real estate-led

Pehla hai real estate-led approach. Yahan company ka core kaam ghar banana aur bechna hai. Zameen khareedo, project banao, ghar becho. Kuch companies community ko khud chalati bhi hain, lekin unki kamai ka bada hissa ghar ki bikri se hi aata hai. Ye wahi traditional real estate capex model hai, bas customer buzurg hai.

**DLF**, market cap ke hisaab se India ki sabse badi listed real estate company hai. 2026 mein DLF ne Gurugram mein apna pehla senior living project, The Aureva, launch kiya. Company iss project se 2,000 crore ka sales target kar rahi thi. Iss project mein 45 floors ka ek tower hai, jisme 4 BHK ke 172 flats hain, jinka average ticket size 11.5 crore hai. Launch ke 48 ghante ke andar company ko 406 confirmed expressions of interest mile, yaani har flat ke liye lagbhag 2.4 khareedar. Aur as of October 2026, saare flats book ho gaye, lagbhag 1,985 crore mein.

**Ashiana Housing**, ek listed real estate company hai jo 1979 se ghar bana rahi hai, aur Bharat mein senior living ki pioneer maani jaati hai. Company ne apna pehla senior living project, Ashiana Utsav, 2007 mein Bhiwadi mein launch kiya tha. Ashiana ghar banati bhi hai, bechti bhi hai, aur community khud chalati bhi hai.

Company ke portfolio mein senior living ka contribution badhta ja raha hai. Paanch saal pehle company ki senior living bookings lagbhag 100 crore thi. FY26 me ye 570 crore ho gayi, aur sirf ek saal me FY25 ke 383 crore se lagbhag 49% badhi. FY21 ke aas paas company ke business mix me senior living ka share 10% tha. FY26 mein ye badh ke 23% ho gaya hai. Whole-time director Varun Gupta ke hisaab se agle 2-3 saal me mix "decisively" senior living ki taraf shift hoga, aur company ke paas aage ke liye jo 50 lakh square feet zameen hai, "the entire 5 million square feet is senior living."

Company ne Pune mein senior living ke liye 28.55 acre ki zameen khareedi hai. Ye unki abtak ki sabse badi land deal hai. Iss project mein lagbhag 20 lakh square feet develop hoga, jisse company ko 1,800 se 2,000 crore ki revenue ki ummeed hai. Aur CFO Vikas Dugal ke hisaab se, jab senior living ki sales lagbhag 1,500 crore ke "critical mass" tak pahunchegi, tab operating leverage se margins aur return on equity dono behtar honge.

Iske alawa Pune ki **Paranjape Schemes** apne Athashri brand ke saath 16 senior communities bana chuki hai, jahan 3,000 se zada buzurg rehte hain. Hyderabad mein Saket Engineers ka **Saket Pranamam** bhi isi model pe chalta hai. Aur **Hiranandani Group** Chennai ke paas Oragadam mein 400 ghar ka premium 'Elements' project la raha hai.

Toh real estate-led approach mein, Ashiana, Paranjape ka Athashri aur Saket Pranamam jaisi companies mid-market aur Tier 2 customers ko target kar rahi hain, jahan ek ghar lagbhag 1 crore ke aas paas hai. Aur DLF aur Hiranandani jaisi companies metro cities ke premium aur luxury end ko target kar rahi hain, jahan DLF ka ek ghar 11.5 crore ka hai.

## Approach 2: Integrated

Doosra hai integrated approach. Yahan ghar sirf product ka ek hissa hai. Company ghar ke saath community operations, hospitality aur healthcare ko bhi ek package mein deti hai. Aur aksar ye kaam do companies milke karti hain: ek developer jo ghar banata hai, aur ek specialist operator jo use chalata hai.

**Max Group** iska sabse clear example hai, kyunki usne ye model literally do listed companies mein baant diya hai. Max Estates ghar banati aur bechti hai, aur Max India ki subsidiary Antara Senior Living unhe chalati hai. Max Estates ne Gurugram ke Estate 360 mein 292 senior homes 11 mahine mein bech diye, lagbhag 1,530 crore mein, yaani ek ghar lagbhag 5 crore ka. Uske baad December 2025 mein Estate 361 launch hua, jahan March 2026 tak phase 1 ke 180 mein se 127 ghar bik chuke the.

Aur yahan operator ki kamai dekho. Max India ke June 2026 investor presentation ke hisaab se Antara ko project value ka 8-10% project management fee milti hai. Q4 FY26 earnings call mein management ne bataya ki Estate 360 se lagbhag 130 crore aur Estate 361 se lagbhag 200 crore ki management fee expected hai, jispe direct variable cost sirf lagbhag 15% hai. Iske alawa Antara ke paas 4 shehron mein 485 care home beds bhi hain. Yaani Max ka model ghar se lekar care tak, poori chain cover karta hai.

**Primus Senior Living** iss approach ka doosra bada naam hai. Primus Brigade, Wadhwa, Dosti Realty, Srijan aur Naiknavare jaise developers ke saath 6 shehron mein 9 projects pe kaam kar rahi hai. Brigade ke saath iske 3 projects mein 600 se zada ghar hain, jinka gross development value lagbhag 750 crore hai. Aur Primus ne HDFC Capital ke saath 2,000 crore ka senior living platform banaya hai. Primus ka ek interesting concept hai multi-generational living, jahan maa-baap ek serviced senior apartment mein rehte hain, aur bachche bagal wale normal tower mein.

**Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities** 6 shehron mein lagbhag 3,000 crore invest kar rahi hai, aur 13 projects mein lagbhag 2,600 ghar bana rahi hai, 70 lakh se 1.5 crore ki range mein. Ye Embassy, TVS Emerald aur Asset Homes jaise local developers ke saath kaam karti hai, aur abhi 10 communities mein lagbhag 1,700 ghar manage karti hai. Serene ne Bengaluru mein 104 beds ki assisted living facility bhi shuru ki hai, jahan medical care ke saath ek 1BHK ka rent 50,000 se 75,000 rupaye mahina hai, aur medical part KITES Senior Care sambhalti hai.

**Godrej Properties** ne bhi khud operator banne ke bajaye North Bengaluru mein Manasum Senior Living ke saath 'Banyan' project shuru kiya hai, ek luxury intergenerational community.

Aur iss approach ke ek kone pe healthcare-led players bhi hain, jaise **Athulya**, **KITES** aur **Epoch Elder Care**, jinka focus ghar se zada assisted living, rehabilitation aur dementia care pe hai. Yahan tak ki Ashiana, jo real estate-led hai, bhi assisted living aur dementia care ke liye Epoch ke saath kaam karti hai.

Toh integrated approach mein, Serene aur Primus jaise players mid-market ko target kar rahe hain, jahan ghar lagbhag 70 lakh se 1.5 crore ka hai. Aur Max Group ka Antara, Godrej-Manasum aur Athulya jaise players premium aur luxury end ko target kar rahe hain.

Lekin sawaal ye hai dosto, ki agar real estate-led model mein ghar itni tezi se bik rahe hain, toh companies integrated approach ki taraf kyu jaa rahi hain? Kyunki sirf buildings ban jaane se senior care ensure nahi ho jata. Seniors ki comfortable living ke liye 24 ghante emergency response, nurse aur doctor tak turant pahunch, unki sehat ke hisaab se khana, aur akelepan se bachne ke liye ek community bhi zaroori hai.

### Ye shift senior care ke liye zaroori kyu hai

Iska simple reason ye hai ki senior living mein building asli product nahi hai. Asli product service hai. Ek family jab 5 crore ka senior home khareedti hai, toh voh teen bedroom ke paise nahi de rahi. Voh paise de rahi hai iss bharose ke, ki raat 2 baje emergency hui toh nurse aayegi, aur ambulance "golden hour" ke andar bade hospital tak pahunchegi. Colliers ki report ye seedha kehti hai, senior living "not a build-and-sell product rather it is an operating business."

Doosra, developer ki kamai ghar ke handover pe khatam ho jaati hai. Lekin buzurg ki care ki zaroorat uske baad agle 15-20 saal tak badhti hai. Agar koi community ko achhe se chalaye hi nahi, toh wahi ghar kuch saal baad bekaar ho jaata hai. Aur senior home sirf doosre senior ko hi resale ho sakta hai, aur woh bhi tab, jab community achhe se chal rahi ho.

Teesra, iss market mein bharosa sab kuch hai. Colliers ke hisaab se ek trusted operator ke 30-50% naye residents referrals se aate hain: purane residents, doctors aur nurses ke through. Ye reputation building se nahi, service se banti hai.

Aur chautha, sabse bada gap bhi yahin hai. Part 1 mein humne dekha tha ki Bharat mein abhi sirf lagbhag 2,100 assisted living beds hain, jabki 2030 tak lagbhag 11,000 ki zaroorat hogi. Ye gap sirf ghar bana ke nahi bharega.

### Doosre deshon mein kya hua

Jin deshon mein senior living mature ho chuka hai, wahan ye shift bahut pehle ho chuka hai.

America mein "active adult" rental communities ek alag asset class ban gayi hain. Log ghar khareedte nahi, rent pe rehte hain, aur company services se kamati hai. UK mein pension funds iss business ke maalik ban gaye. Late 2025 mein NatWest Pension Trustees ne Legal & General se Inspired Villages ka 100% hissa khareed liya, ek chalte hue senior living business ko lambe samay tak hold karne ke liye. China mein insurance companies hi developer ban gayi. Taikang Insurance mein aap ek whole-life policy khareedte ho, aur retirement pe cash ke bajaye aapko ya aapke maa-baap ko Taikang ki premium retirement community mein guaranteed jagah milti hai. Aur Japan ne ise sarkari long-term care insurance ke through poore system ka hissa bana diya.

Har jagah pattern ek hi hai. Building ka maalik ek lamba sochne wala investor hota hai, jaise pension fund, insurer ya REIT. Aur use chalata hai ek specialist operator. Bilkul hotels ki tarah, jahan building kisi ki hoti hai aur brand kisi aur ka. Colliers ke hisaab se Bharat abhi bhi developer-led phase mein hai, aur agle 5 saal mein developer-operator partnerships mainstream ho jayengi.

### Real estate companies ke liye integrated approach kyu sense banata hai

Ab sawaal ye hai ki agar ghar itni tezi se bik hi rahe hain, toh developer services ke jhanjhat mein kyu pade? Iska jawab numbers mein hai.

Pehla, ek baar ki kamai ke upar recurring kamai. Antara ki management fee hum dekh chuke hain. Iske upar maintenance income pe steady state mein 20-25% EBITDA margin, aur jab bhi ghar resale ho, tab resale fee. Antara ke Dehradun project mein FY26 mein 5 ghar resale hue, aur company ne inse lagbhag 1.71 crore kamaye. Aur ye sab, zameen mein ek rupaya lagaye bina.

Doosra, ek stabilised community apne aap mein profitable hai. Antara ki sabse purani Dehradun community ne FY26 mein lagbhag 6.65 crore ka revenue kiya, aur company ke hisaab se voh cash aur profit surplus mein hai.

Teesra, services se ghar jaldi aur mehenge bikte hain. Max India ke hisaab se aise projects mein 95% se zada ghar construction ke dauraan hi bik jaate hain. Yaani customer ka advance hi building banata hai. Aur ek trusted operator ka brand developer ko premium pricing deta hai.

Aur chautha, ye business ko scale deta hai. Ashiana ke CFO ki baat yaad karo. Senior living jab critical mass pe pahunchegi, tab operating leverage se margins aur ROE dono badhenge.

### Ghar bechne ke alawa naye models

Integrated approach ek aur darwaza kholta hai: ghar beche bina kamai. Serene ki assisted living facility mein ghar becha nahi jaata, monthly rent liya jaata hai. Colliers ke hisaab se Prestige Group bhi rent model pe hospitality-led senior living mein aane ki planning kar raha hai, aur iske liye alag leadership team hire ki hai. Aur aage deferred ownership jaise models bhi aa sakte hain, jahan buzurg ghar ki value ka 50-70% refundable deposit dete hain, zindagi bhar rehte hain, aur baad mein deposit unke heirs ko wapas milta hai. Ye sab models tabhi chal sakte hain jab koi community ko lambe samay tak chala raha ho.

### Lekin ye raasta aasaan nahi hai

Ab doosra pehlu bhi dekhna zaroori hai. Services ka business dheere paisa banata hai. Colliers ke hisaab se ek senior living project ko stable occupancy tak pahunchne mein 4 se 6 saal lagte hain.

Aur care, jo sabse zaroori layer hai, abhi sabse mushkil bhi hai. Max India ke care homes ka revenue Q4 FY26 mein lagbhag 11.4 crore raha, saal dar saal 1.6 guna. Lekin 485 beds mein average occupancy sirf 27% thi. Isi wajah se Max India ka EBITDA loss FY25 ke lagbhag 99 crore se ghat ke FY26 mein lagbhag 83 crore hua. Direction sahi hai, lekin company abhi bhi loss mein hai. Management ke hisaab se scale pe care homes ka EBITDA margin 18-20% aur return on capital lagbhag 25-26% ho sakta hai. Lekin abhi ye promise hai, result nahi.

Iske peeche ek cultural wajah bhi hai. Max India ke presentation mein likha hai, "kids pay, parents use." Paisa bachche dete hain, lekin rehna maa-baap ko hai. Aur maa-baap ko apna ghar chhod ke shift hone ke liye manana aaj bhi mushkil hai.

Toh picture kuch aisi hai dosto. Ghar bechne wala business aaj profit kama raha hai. Ghar chalane wala business, khaaskar care, abhi loss mein hai, lekin long-term value wahin hai. JLL ke hisaab se agle 3-5 saal mein rental aur service-led models outright sale ko overtake kar sakte hain. Aur jo company care ko profitably deliver kar payegi, wahi iss sector mein long term mein jeetegi.
