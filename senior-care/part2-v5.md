## 2.1 Moves: kaun kitna paisa laga raha hai

Doston, pichhle ek saal me senior living me paisa tezi se aaya hai. Pehle ek nazar latest moves pe.

DLF ne Gurugram me apna pehla senior living project launch kiya, jisse lagbhag 2,000 crore ki sales ka target tha. Ashiana Housing ne Pune me senior living ke liye 28.55 acre zameen khareedi, jo uski ab tak ki sabse badi land deal hai. Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities 6 shehron me lagbhag 3,000 crore invest kar rahi hai. Primus Senior Living ne HDFC Capital ke saath 2,000 crore ka senior living platform banaya hai. Brigade ne Primus ke saath 750 crore ke projects announce kiye. Max Estates ne Gurugram me Estate 361 launch kiya. Aur Hiranandani aur Godrej Properties jaise naye naam bhi iss segment me utar aaye hain.

Lekin in sab me se do companies ki kahani sabse zada batati hai ki ye business asal me kaisa dikhta hai. Ek luxury end pe hai, aur ek mid-market pe.

### DLF: luxury end

DLF, market cap ke hisaab se India ki sabse badi listed real estate company hai. October 2026 mein DLF ne Gurugram ke Sector 63 mein apna pehla senior living project, The Aureva, launch kiya. Company ne iss project ka alag capex disclose nahi kiya hai, lekin August 2026 ke earnings call ke time iss project se lagbhag 2,000 crore ki sales ki ummeed batayi thi. Iss project mein 45 floors ka ek tower hai, jisme 4 BHK ke 172 flats hain, jinka average ticket size lagbhag 11.5 crore hai. Launch ke 48 ghanton ke andar company ko 406 confirmed expressions of interest mile, yaani har flat ke liye lagbhag 2.4 khareedar. Aur as of October 2026, company ke saare flats book ho gaye, lagbhag 1,985 crore mein. Inme se lagbhag 25% buyers NRI the.

Ye number ek important baat batata hai. Senior home aksar buzurg khud nahi, unke bachche khareedte hain, aur bahut baar videsh me baithe bachche. DLF Home Developers ke MD aur Chief Business Officer Aakash Ohri ne kaha, "We believe The Aureva establishes a new benchmark in this emerging category."

### Ashiana Housing: mid-market end

Doosri taraf hai Ashiana Housing, India ki sabse purani senior living developers mein se ek, jo NSE aur BSE pe listed hai. FY26 mein company ka total revenue lagbhag 1,185 crore aur profit lagbhag 118 crore raha, yaani net margin lagbhag 10%.

Isme senior living ki bikri 570 crore rahi. Ye FY25 ke 383 crore se lagbhag 49% zada hai, aur paanch saal pehle ke lagbhag 100 crore se 5 guna se zada. Company ke business me senior living ka hissa 10% se badh ke 23% ho gaya hai. Company ne FY26 mein 569 senior homes beche, jinka average ticket size lagbhag 1 crore hai, DLF ke ek ghar ki keemat ka lagbhag ek-dasvaan hissa.

Aur company ka iraada saaf hai. Pune ki 28.55 acre zameen uske baad aayi, aur whole-time director ke hisaab se agle 2-3 saal mein business mix "decisively" senior living ki taraf shift hoga. Yaani ek listed developer apna core business hi badal raha hai.

### Ek product, do market

Ab dono ko saath rakh ke dekho. Ashiana ka ghar lagbhag 1 crore. Max Estates ke Estate 360 ka lagbhag 5 crore. DLF ka lagbhag 11.5 crore. Ek hi category me 10 guna se zada ka fark.

Matlab senior living me do bilkul alag market ban rahe hain. Ek mid-market jo Tier 2 aur peripheral shehron me hai, jahan volume hai. Aur ek luxury market jo metros ke andar hai, bade hospitals aur bachchon ke paas, jahan ticket size hai. Aur dono me ghar bik rahe hain.

### Ye kis tarah ka capex hai

Ab in sab moves me ek common baat notice karo. Ye sab classic real estate capex hai. Zameen khareedo, approval lo, building banao, ghar becho. Paisa zameen aur construction me lagta hai, aur wapas ek hi baar aata hai, jab ghar bikta hai.

Aur ye model cash ke maamle me bohot efficient hai. Max India ke investor presentation ke hisaab se aise projects me 95% se zada ghar construction ke dauraan hi bik jaate hain. Yaani customer ka advance hi building banata hai. Developer ke liye senior living bas ek naya premium product hai, usi balance sheet pe.

Toh sawaal ye hai doston. Agar ghar itni tezi se bik rahe hain, toh problem kya hai?

## 2.2 Build-and-sell model ki problem

Problem ye hai ki senior living me building asli product nahi hai. Asli product service hai.

Ek family jab 5 crore ka senior home khareedti hai, toh voh teen bedroom ke paise nahi de rahi. Voh paise de rahi hai uske andar milne wali cheezon ke: raat 2 baje aane wali nurse, on-call doctor, diabetic ke liye sahi khana, emergency me "golden hour" ke andar bade hospital tak pahunchne wali ambulance, aur baat karne ke liye log. Colliers ki report ye seedha kehti hai: senior living "not a build-and-sell product rather it is an operating business."

Aur isse ek developer ke liye, jo banata hai, bechta hai aur nikal jaata hai, chaar problems hoti hain.

**Pehli, kamai wahi khatam hoti hai jahan zaroorat shuru hoti hai.** Developer ki kamai handover pe ruk jaati hai. Lekin buzurg ki care ki zaroorat agle 15-20 saal tak badhti hai. Aur us poore samay ki value usko milti hai jo community chalata hai, usko nahi jisne banayi.

**Doosri, bharosa operator pe hota hai, builder pe nahi.** Families senior living reputation dekh ke chunti hain. Colliers ke hisaab se ek trusted operator ke 30-50% naye residents referrals se aate hain: purane residents, doctors aur nurses ke through. Jo developer agle project pe chala gaya, voh ye reputation nahi bana sakta.

**Teesri, ghar bechne se market chhota rehta hai.** 1-5 crore ka ghar zadatar buzurgon ki pahunch ke bahar hai, aur Bharat me budhape me rent pe rehna aaj bhi stigma hai. Yahi ek badi wajah hai ki 17 lakh affluent households me se sirf 1.5% tak organised senior living pahunchi hai.

**Chauthi, ghar ki value service pe tiki hai.** Senior home sirf doosre senior ko hi resale ho sakta hai, aur woh bhi tab, jab community achhe se chal rahi ho. Service kharab hui toh occupancy giregi, resale value giregi, aur brand ko nuksaan hoga.

Simple shabdon me, build-and-sell model iss sector ka sabse aasaan paisa ek baar kama leta hai, aur long-term value table pe chhod deta hai.

Isiliye ab companies ek doosre tarah ka capex bhi kar rahi hain. Ghar banane me nahi, ghar chalane me.

## 2.3 Operations ki taraf shift

### Operations model kya hai

Operations model me developer ghar banata aur bechta hai. Aur ek specialist operator community chalata hai: maintenance, khana, activities, security, emergency response, aur nursing. Operator ko zameen me paisa nahi lagana padta. Woh fees aur recurring income kamata hai.

Max Group iska sabse clear example hai, kyunki usne ye model literally do listed companies me baant diya hai. Max Estates ghar banati aur bechti hai. Aur Max India ki subsidiary Antara Senior Living unhe chalati hai.

### Operator kaise kamata hai

Max India ke June 2026 investor presentation ke hisaab se Antara teen tarah se kamata hai.

Pehla, project management fee, project value ka 8-10%. Q4 FY26 earnings call me management ne bataya ki Estate 360 se lagbhag 130 crore aur Estate 361 se lagbhag 200 crore ki management fee expected hai. Aur CFO Ajay Agarwal ke hisaab se iss pe direct variable cost sirf lagbhag 15% hai.

Doosra, maintenance income, jispe steady state pe 20-25% EBITDA margin hai. Antara ki Dehradun community, jo sabse purani hai, ne FY26 me lagbhag 6.65 crore ka revenue kiya, aur company ke hisaab se voh cash aur profit surplus me hai. Yaani ek stabilised community apne aap me profitable hai.

Teesra, resale fee. Dehradun me FY26 me 5 ghar resale hue, aur Antara ne inse lagbhag 1.71 crore kamaye.

Socho, operator ne zameen me ek rupaya nahi lagaya, phir bhi teen jagah se kama raha hai, aur jab tak community chalegi, kamata rahega.

### Naye payment models

Operator hone ka ek aur fayda hai. Woh aise payment models la sakta hai jo market ko sirf ghar khareedne walon se aage badha sakte hain. Colliers teen models batati hai.

Deferred ownership, jahan buzurg ghar ki value ka 50-70% refundable deposit dete hain, zindagi bhar rehte hain, aur baad me deposit unke heirs ko wapas milta hai. Subscription, jahan base price kam hota hai, aur khana, housekeeping ya 24 ghante nursing alag tiers me milti hai. Aur buyback, jahan operator ghar wapas khareedne ya resell karne ki guarantee deta hai.

In teeno me ek baat common hai. Ye tabhi chal sakte hain jab koi community ko lambe samay tak chala raha ho.

### Doosre desh kya kar rahe hain

Jin deshon me senior living mature hai, wahan ye shift pehle hi ho chuka hai.

America me "active adult" rental communities ek alag asset class ban gayi hain, aur communities shehron ke bahar se andar, vertical buildings me aa gayi hain. UK me pension funds maalik ban gaye. Late 2025 me NatWest Pension Trustees ne Legal & General se Inspired Villages ka 100% hissa khareed liya, ek operating senior living business ko lambe samay tak hold karne ke liye. China me insurance companies developer ban gayi. Taikang Insurance me aap ek whole-life policy khareedte ho. Retirement pe cash nahi milta. Uske badle aapko ya aapke maa-baap ko Taikang ki premium retirement community me guaranteed admission milta hai. Aur Japan ne ise sarkari system bana diya, long-term care insurance ke through, aur aisa network jahan care ghar se 30 minute ke andar mile.

Har jagah pattern ek hi hai. Building ka maalik ek patient, long-term investor hota hai, jaise pension fund, insurer ya REIT. Aur use chalata hai ek specialist operator. Bilkul hotels ki tarah, jahan building kisi ki hoti hai aur brand kisi aur ka.

Bharat me abhi tak aisa koi bada private equity acquisition nahi hua. Colliers ke hisaab se Bharat abhi bhi developer-led phase me hai, aur agle 5 saal me developer-operator partnerships mainstream ho jayengi.

### Bharat me kaun aage badh raha hai

Aur Bharat me ye shift shuru ho chuka hai.

Primus Senior Living 6 shehron me 9 projects pe kaam kar rahi hai, aur Brigade, Wadhwa, Dosti Realty, Srijan aur Naiknavare jaise developers ke saath partner hai. Primus ne HDFC Capital ke saath 2,000 crore ka senior living platform banaya hai. Ye iss sector me institutional paise ke sabse bade signals me se ek hai.

Serene Communities 10 communities me lagbhag 1,700 ghar manage karti hai. Bengaluru ke Electronic City me usne 104 beds ki assisted living facility bhi shuru ki hai, jahan medical care ke saath ek 1BHK ka rent 50,000 se 75,000 rupaye mahina hai.

Godrej Properties ne khud operator banne ke bajaye Manasum ke saath partnership ki. Prestige Group, Colliers ke hisaab se, rent model pe hospitality-led senior living me aane ki planning kar raha hai, aur iske liye alag leadership team hire ki hai. Ek bada developer jo ghar bechne ke bajaye rent pe chalana chahta hai. Aur Ashiana, jo khud communities chalati hai, bhi assisted living aur dementia care ke liye Epoch Elder Care ke saath partner karti hai.

### Lekin operations aasaan nahi hai

Ab doosra pehlu bhi dekhna zaroori hai, kyunki numbers ye bhi dikhate hain.

Operations dheere paisa banata hai. Colliers ke hisaab se ek senior living project ko feasibility se stable occupancy tak pahunchne me 4 se 6 saal lagte hain. Aur Bharat ka real estate capital jaldi return chahta hai.

Aur care, jo sabse zaroori layer hai, abhi sabse mushkil hai. Max India ke care home business ka revenue Q4 FY26 me lagbhag 11.4 crore raha, saal dar saal 1.6 guna. 485 beds, 4 shehron me. Lekin average occupancy sirf 27% thi, jo pichhle quarter ke 29% se bhi kam hai, kyunki naye beds add hue.

Iska asar poori company pe dikhta hai. Max India ka EBITDA loss FY25 ke lagbhag 99 crore se ghat ke FY26 me lagbhag 83 crore hua. Direction sahi hai, lekin company abhi bhi loss me hai. Aur FY26 me consolidated loss lagbhag 122 crore raha, jisme Noida authority ke saath lagbhag 176 crore ka settlement bhi shamil hai.

Management phir bhi optimistic hai. Care home business ke CEO ke hisaab se scale pe care homes ka EBITDA margin 18-20% aur return on capital lagbhag 25-26% ho sakta hai. Aur MD Rajit Mehta ke hisaab se agle 9 se 12 mahine me "significant growth" expected hai. Lekin abhi ye promise hai, result nahi.

Iske peeche ek cultural wajah bhi hai. Max India ke presentation me likha hai, "kids pay, parents use." Paisa bachche dete hain, rehna maa-baap ko hai. Aur maa-baap ko apna ghar chhodne ke liye manana aaj bhi mushkil hai.

Toh picture saaf hai doston. Ghar bechne wala business aaj profit kama raha hai: Ashiana ka margin dekho, DLF ka sold-out tower dekho. Ghar chalane wala business, khaaskar care, abhi loss me hai, lekin long-term value wahin hai. JLL ke hisaab se agle 3-5 saal me rental aur service-led models outright sale ko overtake kar sakte hain.

Aur Part 1 ka number yaad karo. Assisted living ke abhi sirf 2,100 beds hain, aur 2030 tak lagbhag 11,000 ki zaroorat hogi. Sabse bada gap wahin hai jahan model sabse mushkil hai. Aur jo company care ko profitably deliver kar payegi, wahi iss sector me long term me jeetegi.
