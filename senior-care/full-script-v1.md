## Part 1: India's Ageing Economy

Aaj ke din Bharat ki jansankhya ka lagbhag 11% senior citizen ki category mein aata hai. Aur 2050 tak ye number dugna ho kar 34.6 crore ho jaega. Isme bhi 75 saal se upar ki population zada tezi se badhegi. JLL ke hisaab se 75 saal se upar ki population har saal 7.8% badh rahi hai. Yaani, 2050 tak har 5 mein se 1 Bhartiya senior citizen hoga. In fact UNFPA ki India Ageing Report 2023 ke hisaab se, 2046 ke aaspaas Bharat mein buzurgon ki sankhya bachchon se zyada ho jaegi.

Aisa mostly declining fertility rates aur increased life expectancy ki wajah se ho raha hai.

Aur dosto, iss age group ke logo ki kaafi saari vulnerabilities hoti hain. Inhe dementia, Alzheimer's jaise health conditions se deal karna padta hai. NITI Aayog ki 2024 ki 'Senior Care Reforms in India' report ke hisaab se lagbhag 75% buzurgon ko koi na koi chronic disease hai. Aur Max India ke data ke hisaab se 24% buzurg rozmarra ke kaam khud nahi kar paate hain.

Limited financial resources aur badhte living aur medical expenses se bhi deal karna padta hai. Sath hi, Bharat mein social security provisions bhi insufficient hain. UNFPA ke hisaab se lagbhag 18.7% buzurgon ki koi income hi nahi hai, aur 40% se zyada sabse gareeb 20% gharon mein aate hain. Aur sarkari old age pension scheme mein Centre 60 se 79 saal ke buzurg ko sirf 200 rupaye mahina deta hai, aur 80 ke upar walon ko 500 rupaye. Aur ye amount 2007 se nahi badla hai.

[ON SCREEN: ₹200/month (60–79) | ₹500/month (80+) | unchanged since 2007 | ~2.2 crore beneficiaries]

Dosto, pehle to usually joint families mein parivar ke log hi buzurgon ka khyal rakh lete the. Lekin ab Bharat ka family structure bhi badal raha hai. Max India ke investor presentation ke hisaab se, aaj 70% families nuclear hain. Bachche padhai aur naukri ke liye doosre shehron ya videsh chale jaate hain. Toh joint family wala jo default care system tha, voh tootta jaa raha hai. Aur iska asar akelepan me dikhta hai. HelpAge India ke 2025 ke survey me 10 shehron ke lagbhag 47% buzurgon ne loneliness ko ek serious problem bataya.

Jis wajah se senior care ki need Bharat mein bhi ab badh rahi hai. Government estimates ke hisaab se, 2024 mein Bharat ki silver economy lagbhag 73,000 crores ki thi. Aur 2030 tak ye market lagbhag 1 trillion rupees tak ka ho sakta hai. Iss market ko hum do segments mein baant sakte hain.

Ek market hai jaha need hai par accessibility nahi hai. Humaare desh mein ek taraf 78% buzurgon ke paas koi pension nahi hai. Aur sirf 18% ke paas health insurance hai.

Doosri taraf aate hain affluent urban seniors. Jo retired hain, lekin financially independent hain. Jinke bachche bahar settled hain, aur jo 1-5 crore ka ghar khareed sakte hain. JLL ke hisaab se, overall senior population me 17 lakh seniors aise hain. Aur 2030 tak ye badh kar 21 lakh tak pohoch jaenge. Zyadatar companies abhi isi market ko target kar rahi hain.

Yaani iss segment ki demand to badh rahi hai. Aur aage aur bhi tezi se badhegi.

Lekin Bharat mein abhi organised senior care living units kaafi kam hain. Aur jo existing facilities hain bhi wo 80-85% occupancy par operate kar rahi hain. JLL ke hisaab se aaj ke din Bharat mein sirf 25,050 organised senior living units hain. Yaani supply abhi addressable market ka sirf 1.5% cater kar pa rahi hai. Wahi America mein ye number 6-7% hai, aur New Zealand mein 14-15%.

To isi demand supply gap ko dekhte huye kayi real estate companies, aur government, dono ne ab iss senior care segment pe focus karna shuru kar diya hai. JLL ke hisaab se, organised senior living supply annually 8.5% se grow kar rahi thi. Aur 2024 se June 2026 ke beech ye growth 14.2% per year ho gayi thi.

Chaliye inn moves ko detail mein samajhte hain.

## Part 2: What are Companies Doing in the Segment?

Senior care industry mein real-estate companies majorly do approaches follow kar rahi hain. Pehla hai pure play real-estate mode jaha companies ka role sirf real estate projects build karne tak hi seemit hai. Aur doosra hai ek integrated approach. Jaha companies real estate projects ke sath residents ke health care, caregiving aur community needs par bhi focus kar rahi hain. Chaliye inhe detail mein samajhte hain.

### Pure-Play Real Estate Approach

DLF, market cap ke hisaab se India ki sabse badi listed real estate company hai. 2026 mein DLF ne Gurugram mein apna pehla senior living project, The Aureva, launch kiya. Company ne iss project se lagbhag 2,000 crore ki revenue potential batayi thi. Iss project mein 45 floors ka ek tower involved hai, jisme 4 BHK ke 172 flats hain, jinka average ticket size 11.5 crore hai. 4 October 2026 ko company ne bataya ki saare flats lagbhag 1,985 crore mein bik gaye.

Ashiana Housing, kaafi time se real-estate segment mein active hai, aur 20 saal se zyada se senior living mein hai. Company ke portfolio mein senior living ka contribution badhta ja raha hai. Paanch saal pehle company ki senior living bookings lagbhag 100 crore thi. FY26 me ye 570 crore ho gayi, aur sirf ek saal me FY25 ke 383 crore se lagbhag 49% badhi. Management ke hisaab se paanch saal pehle company ke business me senior living ka share 10% tha. FY26 mein ye badh ke 23% ho gaya hai. Whole-time director ke hisaab se agle 2-3 saal me mix "decisively" senior living ki taraf shift hoga. Company ka target hai ki unka senior living segment long term mein 25% annually grow kare.

Company ne Pune mein senior living ke liye 28.55 acre ki zameen khareedi hai. Ye senior living ke liye company ki abtak ki sabse badi land deal hai. Iss project se company 1,800 se 2,000 crore ki sales expect kar rahi hai.

Iske alawa Pune ki Paranjape Schemes apne Athashri brand ke saath 16 senior communities bana chuki hai, jahan 3,000 se zyada buzurg rehte hain. Hyderabad mein Saket Engineers ka Saket Pranamam bhi isi model pe chalta hai. Aur Hiranandani Group Chennai ke paas Oragadam mein 400 ghar ka premium 'Elements' project la raha hai.

### Integrated Approach

Chaliye ab real-estate companies ke doosre approach ko samajhte hain. Dosto, sirf buildings ban jaane se senior care ensure nahi ho jata hai. Seniors ki comfortable living ke liye caregiver availability, social connection aur healthcare access bhi equally important hain.

Bharat mein senior care abhi tak service-led segment nahi bana hai. Abhi, yaha zyadatar property development par focus kiya ja raha tha. Jabki, China, Japan, US aur UK jaise markets mein properties ke sath healthcare, insurance, financial help aur community building jaise services bhi integrated hain.

China mein Taikang naam ki ek badi insurance company ne senior living ko insurance ke saath jod diya. Aap Taikang se ek whole-life insurance policy khareedte ho. Retirement pe aapko cash ke badle, Taikang ki premium retirement community mein rehne ki guaranteed jagah milti hai. Aur un communities ke andar hi medical care bhi milti hai. Yaani living space, healthcare aur finance teeno ek hi company ke paas hain, toh buzurg ko teen alag jagah bhaagna nahi padta. UK mein bhi aisa hi kuch dikhta hai. Wahan retirement communities sarkari health system NHS ke saath kaam karti hain. Hospital se discharge hue buzurg community ke andar hi recover karte hain, jisse hospital ke beds fatafat khali ho jaate hain. Yaani integrated model mein ghar, care aur paisa ek doosre ko support karte hain.

Bharat mein bhi real estate companies ab aisa hi kuch build karne ki koshish kar rahi hain. Aur companies operators ke sath partnerships ke through iss model mein focus badha rahi hain. Operator woh company hai jo ghar bikne ke baad community ko roz chalati hai. Staff rakhna, khana, saaf safai, maintenance, 24 hour emergency response, aur nurses aur doctors ki availability, ye sab operator sambhalta hai. Real-estate developer ghar banata hai, aur operator usme rehne ka experience deta hai. Badle mein operators, developer se project management fee ya revenue share lete hain, aur residents se monthly maintenance aur service charges collect karte hain. Colliers ke hisaab se aane waale 5 saal mein Bharat mein developer-operator models mainstream ho jaenge.

Chaliye iss model ke kuch examples dekhte hain.

Brigade Group ne Primus Senior Living ke saath 3 projects announce kiye hain, 600 se zyada ghar, jinka gross development value lagbhag 750 crore ka hai. Primus, senior living ke liye retirement aur specialized communities design, build aur manage karta hai. Iss partnership mein, Brigade ka kaam hoga real estate planning and construction, aur Primus healthcare tie-ups, community services, aur daily services ka dhyan rakhegi.

North Bengaluru mein Godrej Properties ki township Godrej Royale Woods ke andar Manasum Senior Living 'Banyan' naam ki community chalati hai, 13 acre mein 191 ghar. Jaha Godrej Properties ne township aur infrastructure banaya, aur Manasum uss community mein healthcare aur roz ki services ensure karti hai.

Max Group ka case thoda unique hai. Group ki ek listed company, Max Estates, ghar banati hai, aur doosri listed company Max India ki subsidiary Antara Senior Living unn projects ko chalati hai. Company ke hisaab se Antara ko project value ka 8-10% management fee mil jaati hai. Sath hi maintenance par EBITDA margin 20-25% hota hai. Aur iske liye Antara ko na zameen khareedni padti hai, na construction ka paisa lagana padta hai. Max Estates ne Gurugram ke Estate 360 mein 11 mahine mein lagbhag 1,530 crore ke ghar bech diye. Company ne December 2025 mein Estate 361 launch kiya. Management ke hisaab se Antara ko in dono projects se lagbhag 330 crore ki management fee milne ki ummeed hai, 130 crore Estate 360 se aur 200 crore Estate 361 se.

Lekin care ka business abhi mushkil hai. Max India ke care homes ka revenue Q4 FY26 mein lagbhag 11.4 crore raha, saal dar saal 1.6 guna. Lekin 485 beds mein average occupancy sirf 27% thi. Isi wajah se Max India ka EBITDA loss FY25 ke lagbhag 99 crore se ghat ke FY26 mein lagbhag 83 crore hua. Direction sahi hai, lekin company abhi bhi loss mein hai. Management ke hisaab se scale pe care homes ka EBITDA margin 18-20% aur return on capital lagbhag 25-26% ho sakta hai.

Iske alawa, unlisted space me bhi kai companies integrated model par kaam kar rahi hain. Primus akele Wadhwa Group, Dosti Realty, Srijan Realty, Naiknavare Developers aur Gopalan Enterprises jaise developers ke saath 6 shehron mein 9 projects pe kaam kar rahi hai, aur usne HDFC Capital ke saath 2,000 crore ka senior living platform bhi banaya hai. Chennai mein Ramaniyam Real Estates aur VNCT Global, Athulya Senior Care ke saath kaam kar rahe hain.

### Sarkar kya kar rahi hai?

Policy mein sabse bada kadam healthcare mein aaya hai. October 2024 se Ayushman Bharat ke andar 70 saal se upar ke har buzurg ko, income chahe kuch bhi ho, 5 lakh rupaye ka free health cover milta hai. Ab tak lagbhag 1.14 crore Vay Vandana cards issue ho chuke hain. April 2024 se IRDAI ne health insurance khareedne ki 65 saal ki age limit bhi hata di.

Housing mein abhi states aage hain. Haryana ne retirement housing policy notify ki hai, jisme 24 ghante ambulance, paas ke super-specialty hospital ke saath tie-up, aur buzurg, developer aur operator ke beech tripartite agreement zaroori hai. Maharashtra ki draft policy senior housing ko zyada FSI aur kam development charges deti hai, lekin 24 ghante nurse aur on-call doctor ko mandatory banati hai.

Lekin national level par abhi koi regulator nahi hai, aur Centre ki nayi National Policy for Senior Citizens abhi draft stage mein hai.

## Part 3: Real estate ke alawa kaun dimaag laga raha hai?

Ghar is economy ka sirf ek hissa hai. Dosto, ghar ban jaane ke baad bhi buzurg ko doctor, nurse, roz ki madad aur paise ka intezaam chahiye. Aur yahan bhi kaafi khiladi aa rahe hain.

**Pehla, healthcare.** Buzurg hospital ke sabse bade customer hain. ASLI-PwC ke hisaab se 70 saal se upar ke logon mein har 1,000 mein se 111 log saal mein hospital mein admit hote hain, jo 30-44 saal walon se 4.3 guna zyada hai. Isliye hospital chains ab ghar tak care pahuncha rahi hain. Max Healthcare ka home care business, Max@Home, Q4 FY26 mein 73 crore ka revenue laaya, saal dar saal 30% zyada.

**Doosra, home care aur non-medical services.** Zyadatar buzurg apna ghar chhodna nahi chahte. Toh startups ghar par hi care de rahe hain. Emoha aur Epoch Elder Care chalane wali Age Care Labs ne July 2026 mein 85 crore raise kiye. Primus ne 2024 mein General Catalyst ki leadership mein 20 million dollar raise kiye the. Aur Primus–HDFC Capital ka 2,000 crore ka platform dikhata hai ki institutional paisa bhi ab iss sector mein aa raha hai.

**Teesra, insurance.** Age limit hatne se buzurg ab nayi health policy le sakte hain. Lekin hospital ke bahar wali long-term care, jaise ghar par nurse ya assisted living, ka kharcha aaj bhi zyadatar jeb se jaata hai. Bharat mein long-term care insurance abhi exist hi nahi karta.

**Chautha, finance.** Kai buzurgon ke paas paisa nahi, ghar hai. Reverse mortgage isi ke liye bana tha, lekin chala nahi. Bank property value ka sirf 35-55% hi dete hain, aur interest 10-11% hota hai, jo normal loan against property se bhi mehenga hai.

**Aur paanchva, workforce.** Care ka sabse bada bottleneck log hain. ASLI-PwC ke hisaab se Bharat ko 1.5 lakh trained caregivers ka target poora karna hai, aur abhi geriatric training medical aur nursing curriculum ka standard hissa bhi nahi hai.

## Conclusion

Toh dosto, picture kuch aisi hai. Demand saaf hai: 2050 tak 34.6 crore buzurg, aur aaj sirf 1.5% penetration. Aur paisa sabse pehle wahan aa raha hai jahan model sabse aasaan hai, yaani ghar banana aur bechna. DLF ne ek tower 1,985 crore mein bech diya.

Lekin senior care mein asli product ghar nahi, care hai. Aur care ka business abhi bhi mushkil hai. Antara ke care homes 27% occupancy par chal rahe hain, aur Max India abhi bhi loss mein hai.

Isliye aane waale saalon mein teen cheezein dekhna zaroori hai. Pehla, kya operators apni occupancy aur margins badha paate hain. Doosra, kya rental aur subscription models aate hain, jisse market sirf crore-pati buzurgon tak seemit na rahe. Aur teesra, kya sarkar ek clear regulatory framework aur long-term care insurance laati hai.

Jo company ghar ke saath care ko bhi profitably deliver kar payegi, wahi iss sector mein lambe samay tak jeetegi.
