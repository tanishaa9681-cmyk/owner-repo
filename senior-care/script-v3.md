## Part 1: India's Ageing Economy

### Buzurg badh rahe hain, family chhoti ho rahi hai

UNFPA ki India Ageing Report ke hisaab se, 2022 me Bharat me 60 saal se upar ke lagbhag 14.9 crore log the. Yaani population ka 10.5%. 2050 tak ye number lagbhag dugna ho kar 34.7 crore ho jayega, yaani har 5 me se 1 Bharatiya senior citizen hoga. Aur 2046 ke aas paas Bharat me buzurgon ki sankhya 0 se 14 saal ke bachchon se zada ho jayegi. JLL ke hisaab se 75 saal se upar ki population har saal 7.8% badh rahi hai, aur yahi woh age group hai jise sabse zada care chahiye.

Lekin sirf buzurgon ki sankhya badhna kaafi nahi hai. Asli badlav family structure me aa raha hai. Max India ke investor presentation ke hisaab se aaj 70% families nuclear hain. Bachche padhai aur naukri ke liye doosre shehron ya videsh chale jaate hain. Toh joint family wala jo default care system tha, voh toot raha hai.

Aur iska asar akelepan me dikhta hai. HelpAge India ke 2025 ke survey me 10 shehron ke lagbhag 47% buzurgon ne loneliness ko ek serious problem bataya.

Iska ek geographic pattern bhi hai. Colliers ki report ke hisaab se jin states se bachche sabse zada bahar jaate hain, jaise Kerala, Punjab, Maharashtra, Gujarat aur Andhra Pradesh, wahan senior communities me interest sabse zada hai. Isiliye Bharat ke 60% se zada organised senior living aur care projects South India me hain.

### Ek nahi, do market

Ab yahan doston ek important baat samajhni hogi. Bharat ki senior population ek market nahi, balki do market hai.

Ek taraf, Max India ka presentation kehta hai ki 78% buzurgon ke paas koi pension nahi hai, aur sirf 18% ke paas health insurance hai. NITI Aayog ke hisaab se 75% se zada buzurgon ko koi na koi chronic bimari hai, aur Max India ke data ke hisaab se 24% buzurg rozmarra ke kaam khud se nahi kar paate. Ye woh market hai jahan zaroorat sabse zada hai, lekin paisa kam.

Doosri taraf hai affluent urban senior. Retired, lekin financially independent. Bachche bahar settled, aur 1-5 crore ka ghar khareedne ki capacity. JLL ke hisaab se aise households 2026 me 17 lakh hain, aur 2030 tak 21 lakh ho jayenge. Abhi zadatar companies issi doosre market ko target kar rahi hain.

### Market kitna bada hai

NITI Aayog ne poori senior care industry ko lagbhag 7 billion dollars ka estimate kiya tha. JLL-ASLI ki September 2026 ki report ke hisaab se sirf senior living ka market 2030 tak 10.1 billion dollars ka opportunity ban sakta hai. Colliers isse aur bada, lagbhag 12 billion dollars ka maanta hai.

Supply bhi tezi se badh rahi hai. 2019 se 2023 ke beech organised senior living supply saalana 8.5% badh rahi thi. 2024 se June 2026 ke beech ye growth 14.2% saalana ho gayi. JLL ke policy-driven scenario me 2030 tak supply 25,000 units se badh ke 74,000 units tak jaa sakti hai, jiske liye lagbhag 7.7 billion dollars ka investment chahiye hoga.

Policy bhi help kar rahi hai. Maharashtra ne Housing Policy 2025 me senior citizen housing ko alag category banaya hai, aur aise ghar khareedne pe stamp duty sirf 1,000 rupaye kar di hai. Haryana ne retirement housing ke liye FAR 2.25 se badha ke 3 kar diya hai, yaani developer usi zameen pe zada built-up area bana sakta hai. Shayad isliye Gurugram aaj Bharat ka sabse hot senior living market hai.

### Toh penetration sirf 1.5% kyu?

Itni demand, itni growth, phir bhi penetration America ka ek-chauthai. Kyu?

Colliers ki report iska seedha jawab deti hai: Bharat me problem supply ki nahi, demand ko activate karne ki hai. Teen wajah hain.

Pehli, decision distress me liya jaata hai. Zadatar families senior living tab dekhti hain jab koi health crisis aata hai, ya bachche videsh chale jaate hain. America me log ise lifestyle choice ki tarah dekhte hain. Bharat me aaj bhi "old age home" sunte hi bachchon ko guilt hota hai.

Doosri, Bharat me ghar pe care sasti hai. Colliers ke hisaab se America me 24 ghante ki private home care bohot mehengi hai, isliye senior living ek logical option ban jaata hai. Bharat me domestic help aasaani se aur kam paise me mil jaati hai. Toh senior living ka competitor koi doosri company nahi, balki ghar ki maid aur driver hain.

Teesri, ye sector abhi unregulated hai. Social Justice Ministry ne guidelines di hain, lekin koi statutory body nahi hai jo enforce kare. Toh families ko samajh hi nahi aata ki kis project pe bharosa karein.

Lekin ek badlav aa raha hai. Colliers ke hisaab se COVID ke baad stigma kam hua hai, aur senior living ka decision lene ki umar late 50s tak aa gayi hai. Yaani log ab crisis ka wait nahi kar rahe. Aur lagbhag 30% buzurg aaj financially independent hain.

Ek aakhri baat yaad rakhna. Assisted living, yaani jahan buzurgon ko roz ki madad aur medical care milti hai, uske Bharat me abhi sirf 2,100 beds hain. 2030 tak lagbhag 11,000 ki zaroorat hogi. Ispe ham wapas aayenge.

## Part 2: Ghar Banana vs Ghar Chalana

Doston, iss sector me companies abhi do tarah ki strategy apna rahi hain. Pehli, build and operate. Doosri, operations. Aur iss part ki poori kahani ye hai ki industry pehli se doosri ki taraf kyu shift ho rahi hai.

### 2.1 Strategy 1: Build and operate, sector ka traditional capex model

Build and operate ka matlab hai, developer zameen khareedta hai, ghar banata hai, bechta hai, aur zarurat pade toh community bhi chalata hai. Ye wahi purana real estate model hai, bas customer buzurg hai. Paisa zameen aur construction me lagta hai, yaani heavy capex, aur kamai zadatar ek baar hoti hai, jab ghar bikta hai.

Iss model ka sabse bada example Ashiana Housing hai. FY26 me company ne senior living me 570 crores ki bikri ki, FY25 ke 383 crores se lagbhag 49% zada. 5 saal pehle senior living Ashiana ke business ka 10% tha, aaj 23% hai. Company ne Pune me apni ab tak ki sabse badi zameen bhi senior living ke liye khareedi hai. Pune ki Paranjape Schemes bhi apne Athashri brand ke saath 16 communities bana chuki hai, jahan 3,000 se zada seniors rehte hain.

Aur ab bade developers bhi aa rahe hain. Max Estates ne Gurugram me Estate 360 ke 292 senior homes 11 mahine me bech diye, total sales lagbhag 1,530 crores. Hiranandani Group ne Chennai ke paas Oragadam me 400 residences ka project 'Elements' announce kiya hai. Aur DLF ka Gurugram project aa raha hai, jahan Colliers ke hisaab se ghar 12 crore se upar ke honge.

Iss model me price ka range dekho. Ashiana lagbhag 7,700 rupaye per square foot. Max Estates 20,000-22,000. DLF lagbhag 40,000. Ek hi category me 5 guna ka fark. Mid-market Tier 2 aur peripheral shehron me hai. Luxury metros ke andar, hospitals aur bachchon ke paas.

### 2.2 Strategy 2: Ghar mat banao, ghar chalao

Doosri strategy hai operations. Yahan company zameen nahi khareedti. Developer zameen aur construction sambhalta hai. Operator community chalata hai: khana, maintenance, activities, security, emergency response, aur care. Aur operator iske badle fee aur recurring income kamata hai.

Max Group ne toh isse literally do companies me baant diya hai. Max Estates ghar banati aur bechti hai. Aur Max India ki subsidiary Antara un ghar ko chalati hai.

Antara ki economics dekho. Max India ke June 2026 investor presentation ke hisaab se Antara ko project pe 8-10% project management fee milti hai. Steady state pe maintenance income pe 20-25% EBITDA margin. Pre-sales 95% se zada, yaani customer ka paisa hi construction fund karta hai. Aur resale fee alag. Antara ke Dehradun project me FY26 me 5 ghar resale hue, aur company ne lagbhag 1.71 crores ki fee kamayi.

Socho, operator ne zameen me ek rupaya nahi lagaya, phir bhi use teen jagah se paisa mil raha hai: fee, maintenance, aur resale.

### 2.3 Ye shift kyu samajh me aata hai

Ab sawaal ye hai ki agar ghar bik hi rahe hain, toh operations ki taraf shift kyu? Chaar wajah hain.

Pehli, recurring income. Developer ek baar kamata hai. Operator tab tak kamata hai jab tak buzurg wahan rehte hain. Colliers ke shabdon me, senior living "build-and-sell product nahi, ek operating business hai." Iski long-term value occupancy aur brand se banti hai, construction margin se nahi.

Doosri, customer operator pe bharosa karta hai, builder pe nahi. Buzurg ghar nahi, care khareed raha hai. Colliers ke hisaab se ek trusted operator ke 30-50% naye residents referrals se aate hain, purane residents, doctors aur nurses ke through. Yaani marketing ka kharcha kam, aur occupancy zada. Brand builder ka nahi, operator ka banta hai.

Teesri, demand khulne ke liye naye payment models chahiye, aur unhe sirf operator chala sakta hai. Bharat me ghar khareedna mehenga hai, aur budhape me rent pe rehna stigma. Isliye naye models aa rahe hain. Deferred ownership, jahan buzurg property value ka 50-70% deposit dete hain, zindagi bhar rehte hain, aur baad me deposit unke heirs ko wapas milta hai. Subscription, jahan base rent kam hota hai aur khana, housekeeping ya 24 ghante nursing alag tiers me milti hai. Aur buyback, jahan operator ghar wapas khareedne ki guarantee deta hai. In sab me asli product ghar nahi, service hai.

Chauthi, institutional paisa platforms me aata hai, projects me nahi. Ek single project me invest karna risky hai. Lekin ek operator jo 10 projects chala raha hai, uska track record hota hai. Aur yahi hamein doosre deshon ki kahani tak le jaata hai.

### 2.4 Doosre desh kya kar rahe hain

Jin deshon me senior living mature hai, wahan ye shift pehle hi ho chuka hai. Aur har desh ne apne tareeke se kiya.

America me "active adult" rental ek alag asset class ban gaya hai. Log ghar khareedte nahi, rent pe rehte hain. Aur communities shehron ke bahar se shehron ke andar, "vertical senior living" buildings me aa gayi hain.

UK me pension funds aaye. Late 2025 me NatWest Pension Trustees ne Legal & General se Inspired Villages ka 100% hissa khareed liya, ek operating senior living business ko long term hold karne ke liye.

China me insurance companies developer ban gayi. Taikang Insurance ka model dekho. Aap ek whole-life insurance policy khareedte ho. Retirement pe aapko cash nahi milta. Uske badle aapko ya aapke maa-baap ko Taikang ki premium retirement community me guaranteed admission milta hai. Yaani insurance policy hi senior living ka ticket ban gayi.

Aur Japan ne ise sarkari system bana diya. Long-term care insurance, aur aisa network jahan care ghar se 30 minute ke andar mile.

In sab me ek common pattern hai. Zameen aur building ka maalik koi bada, dheere paisa lagane wala investor hai: pension fund, insurer, ya REIT. Aur operator use chalata hai. Bilkul hotels ki tarah, jahan building kisi ki hoti hai aur brand kisi aur ka.

Bharat me abhi tak koi aisa bada private equity acquisition nahi hua. Colliers ke hisaab se Bharat abhi bhi developer-led phase me hai, aur developer-operator partnerships agle 5 saal me mainstream ho jayengi.

### 2.5 Bharat me kaun aage badh raha hai

Aur Bharat me ye shift shuru ho chuka hai. Dekho kaun kaun aa raha hai.

Primus Senior Living iska sabse aggressive example hai. 6 shehron me 9 projects, aur Brigade, Wadhwa, Dosti Realty, Srijan aur Naiknavare jaise developers ke saath partnership. Primus ne HDFC Capital ke saath 2,000 crores ka senior living platform banaya hai. Ye iss sector me institutional paise ke sabse bade signals me se ek hai.

Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities 10 communities me lagbhag 1,700 units manage karti hai, aur Embassy, TVS Emerald aur Asset Homes jaise developers ke saath kaam karti hai.

Godrej Properties ne khud operator banne ke bajaye Manasum Senior Living ke saath partnership ki hai, North Bengaluru me 'Banyan' project ke liye.

Sabse interesting signal Prestige ka hai. Colliers ke hisaab se Prestige rent model pe hospitality-led senior living me aane ki planning kar raha hai, aur iske liye alag leadership team hire ki hai. Ek bada developer, jo ghar bechne ke bajaye rent pe chalana chahta hai.

Aur dekho, Ashiana bhi, jo build and operate ka sabse bada naam hai, assisted living aur dementia care ke liye Epoch Elder Care ke saath partnership karti hai. Yaani build and operate wale bhi care wali layer specialist ko de rahe hain.

### 2.6 Lekin ye shift aasaan nahi hai

Ab doosra pehlu bhi dekhna zaroori hai.

Operations ka model dheere paisa banata hai. Colliers ke hisaab se ek senior living project ko feasibility se steady occupancy tak pahunchne me 4 se 6 saal lagte hain. Aur Bharat ka real estate capital jaldi return chahta hai.

Aur care, jo sabse zaroori layer hai, abhi struggle kar rahi hai. Antara ke paas 4 shehron me 485 care home beds hain. Q4 FY26 me revenue lagbhag 1.6 guna badha, lekin average occupancy sirf 27% thi. Kuch beds naye add hue hain, isliye ye number temporarily kam hai. Lekin isi wajah se Max India FY26 me consolidated level pe abhi bhi EBITDA loss me hai.

Iski jad wahi cultural problem hai. Max India ke presentation me likha hai, "kids pay, parents use." Paisa bachche dete hain, lekin rehna maa-baap ko hai. Aur risk operations ke andar bhi hain: caregivers ka attrition, emergency me hospital tak pahunchne ka "golden hour" miss hona, aur hidden charges jo trust tod dete hain.

Toh shift ki direction saaf hai. JLL ke hisaab se agle 3-5 saal me rental aur service-led models outright sale ko overtake kar sakte hain. Lekin jeet usi ki hogi jo care ko profitably deliver kar paye. Aur yaad hai Part 1 ka number? Assisted living ke 2,100 beds, aur 11,000 ki zaroorat. Sabse bada gap wahin hai jahan model sabse mushkil hai.

## Part 3: Real Estate Ke Bahar Kaun Aa Raha Hai

Doston, ek simple calculation karte hain. Agar JLL ka sabse optimistic scenario bhi sach ho jaaye aur 2030 tak 74,000 senior living units ban jaayein, toh bhi ye 21 lakh affluent households ka sirf 3.5% hoga. Yaani Bharat ke 95% se zada buzurg apne hi ghar me budhe honge.

Aur Part 1 me hamne dekha ki Bharat me ghar pe care sasti hai. Toh elder care ka sabse bada market community ke andar nahi, ghar ke andar hai. Aur yahan bilkul alag players utar rahe hain.

### Healthcare-led operators

Pehla group un companies ka hai jo ghar nahi, care se shuru hoti hain. KITES Senior Care transition care, post-hospitalisation recovery aur dementia support deti hai. Usne Ranjan Pai ke family office se lagbhag 45 crores raise kiye, aur apne beds 340 se 1,000 tak le jaana chahti hai. Chennai ki Athulya aur Geri Care, aur Gurugram ki Epoch, skilled nursing, rehabilitation aur memory care pe focus karti hain. Yahi woh assisted living gap hai jiski baat hamne Part 1 me ki thi.

### Home healthcare

Doosra, home healthcare. NITI Aayog ke hisaab se Bharat ka home healthcare market 2020 me lagbhag 50,840 crores ka tha, aur 2027 tak 1.74 lakh crores tak jaa sakta hai. Aur investors ka paisa bhi yahin jaa raha hai. Portea Medical ne lagbhag 95 million dollars raise kiye hain, Accel aur IFC jaise investors se. HealthCare at Home, yaani HCAH, ne 64 million dollars se zada raise kiye hain, Quadria Capital aur Burman family se.

### Ageing-at-home platforms

Teesra, aise platforms jo buzurgon ko apne hi ghar me surakshit rakhte hain. Age Care Labs, jo Emoha aur Epoch chalati hai, ne July 2026 me 85 crores raise kiye. Emoha ghar pe emergency response, health monitoring aur hospital accompaniment deti hai. Disclosure, iss round me Zerodha ka investment arm Rainmatter bhi investor hai. Samarth Eldercare subscription model pe Tier 1 se Tier 3 shehron tak ghar pe care coordinate karti hai. Anvayaa specially un NRI families ke liye hai jinke maa-baap Bharat me akele rehte hain. Aur Khyaal jaisi startups community aur engagement app bana rahi hain.

Lekin ek interesting pattern dekho. Portea aur HCAH jaise home healthcare players ne 60-95 million dollars raise kiye hain, jabki zadatar care-management startups ne 1-2 million dollars se kam. Yaani investors abhi hospital jaisi services ko ghar tak laane me zada paisa laga rahe hain, sirf coordination me nahi.

### Insurance

Chautha, insurance. April 2024 se IRDAI ne health insurance khareedne ki 65 saal ki age limit hata di hai. Sarkaar ne Ayushman Bharat ke tehet 70 saal se upar ke sabhi buzurgon ko 5 lakh tak ka cover dena shuru kiya. Lekin February 2025 tak lagbhag 6 crore eligible buzurgon me se sirf 47 lakh ne enrol kiya tha.

Aur China ka Taikang model yaad karo. Wahan insurance hi senior living ka ticket ban gaya. Colliers bhi recommend karti hai ki Bharat senior living ko life insurance schemes se jode. Agar aisa hua, toh insurance companies iss sector ke sabse bade players me se ho sakti hain.

### Products aur medication

Aur paanchva, products. Max India ke hisaab se urban Bharat ke lagbhag 1.5 crore seniors chronic bimariyon ke self-care pe saalana lagbhag 40,000 crores kharch karte hain. Max India ka AGEasy brand, jo walkers, BP monitors aur joint pain ke products online bechta hai, iss segment pe 47% gross margin kama raha hai.

## Conclusion

All in all doston, Bharat ki elder care economy abhi shuruaati daur me hai. Demographics clear hain. Buzurg badh rahe hain, families chhoti ho rahi hain, aur akelapan badh raha hai.

Real estate companies ne pehla bada move kar diya hai, aur ghar bik bhi rahe hain. Lekin industry samajh rahi hai ki asli business ghar banana nahi, ghar chalana hai. Isiliye developers operators ke saath partner kar rahe hain, Prestige jaise players rent model dekh rahe hain, aur HDFC Capital jaisa institutional paisa platforms me aa raha hai.

Lekin iss sector ka asli test care hoga. Wahi layer jo sabse zaroori hai, wahi abhi sabse kam profitable hai. Aur 95% se zada buzurg ghar pe hi budhe honge, toh bada opportunity shayad communities me nahi, ghar tak care pahunchane me hai.

Ek risk bhi hai. Agar zadatar developers sirf luxury senior homes pe focus karte rahe, toh supply ek chhote se affluent segment me hi atak sakti hai.

Aapko kya lagta hai? Kya Bharatiya families apne maa-baap ke liye senior living ya care homes ko accept karengi? Ya ghar pe care hi default rahega? Apne thoughts comments me zarur batana. Mai hun Shantanu Jain, milta hun aapse next Saturday.
