## Pure-Play Real Estate Approach

DLF, market cap ke hisaab se India ki sabse badi real estate company hai. 2026 mein DLF ne Gurugram mein apna pehla senior living project, The Aureva, launch kiya hai. Company iss project se 2,000 crore ka sales target kar rahi thi. Iss project mein 45 floors ka ek tower involved hai, jisme 4 BHK ke 172 flats hai, jinka average ticket size 11.5 crore hai. As of October 2026, company ke saare flats book ho gaye.

Ashiana Housing, kaafi time se real-estate segment mein active hai. Company ne apna pehla senior living project 2007 mein launch kiya tha. Company ke portfolio mein senior living ka contribution badhta ja raha hai. Paanch saal pehle company ki senior living bookings lagbhag 100 crore thi. FY26 me ye 570 crore ho gayi, aur sirf ek saal me FY25 ke 383 crore se lagbhag 49% badhi. FY21 mein company ke business mix me senior living ka share 10% tha. FY26 mein ye badh ke 23% ho gaya hai. Whole-time director ke hisaab se agle 2-3 saal me mix "decisively" senior living ki taraf shift hoga. Company ka target hai ki unka senior care segment 25% annually grow kare.

Company ne Pune mein senior living ke liye 28.55 acre ki zameen khareedi hai. Ye unki abtak ki sabse badi land deal hai. Iss project ke through company 1,800 se 2,000 crore ka revenue expect kar rahi hai.

Iske alawa Pune ki Paranjape Schemes apne Athashri brand ke saath 16 senior communities bana chuki hai, jahan 3,000 se zada buzurg rehte hain. Hyderabad mein Saket Engineers ka Saket Pranamam bhi isi model pe chalta hai. Aur Hiranandani Group Chennai ke paas Oragadam mein 400 ghar ka premium 'Elements' project la raha hai.

## Integrated Approach

Chaliye ab real-estate companies ke doosre approach ko samajhte hain. Dosto, sirf buildings ban jaane se senior care ensure nahi ho jata hai. Seniors ki comfortable living ke liye caregiver availability, social connection aur healthcare access bhi equally important hain.

Bharat mein senior care abhi tak service-led segment nahi bana hai. Abhi, yaha zyadatar property development mein focus kiya ja raha tha. Jabki, China, Japan, US aur UK jaise markets mein properties ke sath healthcare, insurance, financial help aur community building jaise services bhi integrated hain.

China ka example dekho. Wahan Taikang naam ki ek badi insurance company ne senior living ko insurance ke saath jod diya. Aap Taikang se ek whole-life insurance policy khareedte ho. Retirement pe aapko cash nahi milta. Uske badle aapko, ya aapke maa-baap ko, Taikang ki premium retirement community mein guaranteed jagah milti hai. Aur un communities ke andar hi medical care bhi milti hai.

Ab socho ye model itna efficient kyu hai. Insurance ke premium se company ko lambe samay ka paisa pehle se mil jaata hai. Customer pehle se booked hai, toh community khaali nahi rehti. Aur ghar, healthcare aur finance teeno ek hi company ke paas hain, toh buzurg ko teen alag jagah bhaagna nahi padta. UK mein bhi aisa hi kuch dikhta hai. Wahan retirement communities sarkari health system NHS ke saath kaam karti hain, aur hospital se discharge hue buzurg community ke andar hi recover karte hain, jisse hospital ke beds khaali hote hain. Yaani integrated model mein ghar, care aur paisa ek doosre ko support karte hain.

Lekin real estate companies ko caregiving ka experience nahi hota hai. Jis wajah se, ye companies operators ke sath partnerships ke through iss model mein focus badha rahe hain.

Ab operator kaun hota hai? Operator woh company hai jo ghar bikne ke baad community ko roz chalati hai. Staff rakhna, khana, saaf safai, maintenance, activities, 24 ghante emergency response, aur nurse ya doctor ki availability, ye sab operator ka kaam hai. Developer ghar banata hai, aur operator usme rehne ka experience deta hai. Operator kaise kamata hai, ye har deal mein alag hota hai. Jaise Max Group ke case mein, operator Antara ko project pe management fee milti hai, aur residents se har mahine maintenance aur service charges. Kuch partnerships mein operator revenue share leta hai, aur assisted living jaisi facilities mein residents seedha monthly fee dete hain.

Chaliye kuch examples dekhte hain.

Sabse pehle listed companies. Max Group mein ye model do listed companies ke beech bata hua hai. Max Estates ghar banati aur bechti hai, aur Max India ki subsidiary Antara Senior Living unhe chalati hai. Max Estates ne Gurugram ke Estate 360 mein 292 senior homes 11 mahine mein bech diye, lagbhag 1,530 crore mein. December 2025 mein launch hue Estate 361 mein March 2026 tak phase 1 ke 180 mein se 127 ghar bik chuke the. Aur operator ki kamai dekho. Max India ke hisaab se Antara ko project value ka 8-10% management fee milti hai, aur management ke hisaab se Estate 360 aur 361 se milke lagbhag 330 crore ki fee expected hai, jispe direct variable cost sirf lagbhag 15% hai.

Brigade Group ne February 2026 mein Primus Senior Living ke saath 3 projects announce kiye hain, 600 se zada ghar, lagbhag 750 crore ka gross development value. Inme se 2 projects Brigade ki badi townships ke andar honge. Brigade ka kaam hai planning aur construction, aur Primus apna senior living model laayegi: healthcare tie-ups, community programs aur roz ki services. Aur kyunki ye township ke andar hain, maa-baap senior apartment mein, aur bachche usi campus ke normal tower mein reh sakte hain.

Godrej Properties ne North Bengaluru ki Godrej Royale Woods township ke andar Manasum Senior Living ke saath 'Banyan' naam ki senior community bana rahi hai, 13 acre mein 191 ghar. Jaha Godrej Properties township aur infrastructure sambhalega, aur Manasum community chalayegi: 24 ghante paramedical staff, on-call doctor, ambulance, khana aur roz ki services. Aur ye luxury nahi hai, yahan ghar lagbhag 36 lakh se shuru hote hain.

Aur Ashiana Housing, jo khud apni communities chalati hai, wo bhi assisted living aur dementia care ke liye Epoch Elder Care ke saath kaam karti hai. Yaani jo developer khud operations sambhalta hai, wo bhi care ka kaam specialist ko de raha hai.

Iske alawa, unlisted space me bhi kai companies integrated model par kaam kar rahi hain. Primus akele Wadhwa Group, Dosti Realty, Srijan Realty, Naiknavare Developers aur Gopalan Enterprises jaise developers ke saath 6 shehron mein 9 projects pe kaam kar rahi hai, aur usne HDFC Capital ke saath 2,000 crore ka senior living platform bhi banaya hai. Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities Embassy Group, TVS Emerald aur Asset Homes jaise developers ke saath 6 shehron mein lagbhag 3,000 crore invest kar rahi hai. Chennai mein Ramaniyam Real Estates aur VNCT Global, Athulya Senior Care ke saath kaam kar rahe hain. Bengaluru ki Ozone Group aur Mysore ki Tapovan Projects, Covai Care ke saath. Aur Gurugram mein J K Urbanscapes ne Advait Living ke saath premium retirement housing banayi hai.

### Lekin ye raasta aasaan nahi hai

Ab doosra pehlu bhi dekhna zaroori hai. Services ka business dheere paisa banata hai. Colliers ke hisaab se ek senior living project ko stable occupancy tak pahunchne mein 4 se 6 saal lagte hain.

Aur care, jo sabse zaroori layer hai, abhi sabse mushkil bhi hai. Max India ke care homes ka revenue Q4 FY26 mein lagbhag 11.4 crore raha, saal dar saal 1.6 guna. Lekin 485 beds mein average occupancy sirf 27% thi. Isi wajah se Max India ka EBITDA loss FY25 ke lagbhag 99 crore se ghat ke FY26 mein lagbhag 83 crore hua. Direction sahi hai, lekin company abhi bhi loss mein hai. Management ke hisaab se scale pe care homes ka EBITDA margin 18-20% aur return on capital lagbhag 25-26% ho sakta hai. Lekin abhi ye promise hai, result nahi.

Iske peeche ek cultural wajah bhi hai. Max India ke presentation mein likha hai, "kids pay, parents use." Paisa bachche dete hain, lekin rehna maa-baap ko hai. Aur maa-baap ko apna ghar chhod ke shift hone ke liye manana aaj bhi mushkil hai.

Toh picture kuch aisi hai dosto. Ghar bechne wala business aaj profit kama raha hai. Ghar chalane wala business, khaaskar care, abhi loss mein hai, lekin long-term value wahin hai. JLL ke hisaab se agle 3-5 saal mein rental aur service-led models outright sale ko overtake kar sakte hain. Aur jo company care ko profitably deliver kar payegi, wahi iss sector mein long term mein jeetegi.
