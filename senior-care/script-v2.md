# India's Senior Care Economy — Script v2 (Hinglish)



Doston, Bharat ke real estate sector me ek naya segment chupchaap bada ho raha hai. Senior living. Yaani buzurgon ke liye specially design kiye gaye ghar. Aur iss segment me kaafi announcements hue hain. Desh ki sabse badi listed real estate company DLF Gurugram me apna pehla senior living project launch karne wali hai, jisse company lagbhag 2,000 crores kamane ki ummeed kar rahi hai. Max Estates ne Gurugram me 292 senior homes launch kiye the, aur saare ke saare 11 mahine me bik gaye. Average ticket size? Lagbhag 5 crore rupaye. Ashiana Housing ka kehna hai ki uske paas future development ke liye jitni bhi zameen hai, voh poori ki poori senior living ke liye hai. Brigade ne Primus Senior Living ke saath 750 crores ke projects announce kiye hain. Aur Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities 6 shehron me 3,000 crores invest kar rahi hai.

Lekin yahan ek interesting baat hai. JLL aur Association of Senior Living India ki report ke hisaab se, Bharat me lagbhag 17 lakh aise senior households hain jo shehron me rehte hain aur financially independent hain. Lekin unke liye organised senior living units sirf 25,050 hain. Yaani penetration sirf 1.5%. America me ye number 6-7% hai, aur New Zealand me 14-15%.

Fir samjhenge ki real estate companies iss sector me kis strategy se aa rahi hain, aur kiska model kaam kar raha hai. Aur last me dekhenge ki sirf ghar banana kaafi kyu nahi hai. Shuru karte hain.

 ## Part 1: India's Ageing Economy

 UNFPA ki India Ageing Report ke hisaab se, 2022 me Bharat me 60 saal se upar ke lagbhag 14.9 crore log the. Yaani population ka 10.5%. 2050 tak ye number lagbhag dugna ho kar 34.7 crore ho jayega, yaani har 5 me se 1 Bharatiya senior citizen hoga. Aur report ke hisaab se, 2046 ke aas paas Bharat me buzurgon ki sankhya 0 se 14 saal ke bachchon se zada ho jayegi. Ek aur interesting data point. JLL ke hisaab se 75 saal se upar ki population har saal 7.8% badh rahi hai. Aur yahi woh age group hai jise sabse zada care ki zaroorat hoti hai.

 Lekin sirf buzurgon ki sankhya badhna kaafi nahi hai. Asli badlav family structure me aa raha hai. Max India ke investor presentation ke hisaab se, aaj 70% families nuclear hain. Bachche padhai aur naukri ke liye doosre shehron ya videsh chale jaate hain. Toh joint family wala jo default care system tha, voh tootta jaa raha hai.

 Ab yahan doston ek important baat samajhni hogi. Bharat ki senior population ek market nahi, balki do market hai. Ek taraf, wahi presentation kehta hai ki 78% buzurgon ke paas koi pension nahi hai, aur sirf 18% ke paas health insurance hai. NITI Aayog ki report ke hisaab se 75% se zada buzurgon ko koi na koi chronic bimari hai. Aur Max India ke data ke hisaab se 24% buzurg rozmarra ke kaam khud se nahi kar paate. Ye woh market hai jahan zaroorat sabse zada hai, lekin paisa kam.

Doosri taraf hai affluent urban senior. Jo retired hai lekin financially independent hai, jiske bachche bahar settled hain, aur jo 1-5 crore ka ghar khareed sakta hai. JLL ke hisaab se aise households 2026 me 17 lakh hain, aur 2030 tak 21 lakh ho jayenge. Abhi zadatar companies issi doosre market ko target kar rahi hain.

 Toh ye market kitna bada hai? NITI Aayog ne poori senior care industry ko lagbhag 7 billion dollars ka estimate kiya tha. Aur JLL-ASLI ki September 2026 ki report ke hisaab se, sirf senior living ka market 2030 tak 10.1 billion dollars ka opportunity ban sakta hai.

 Aur supply bhi ab tezi se badh rahi hai. 2019 se 2023 ke beech organised senior living supply saalana 8.5% badh rahi thi. Lekin 2024 se June 2026 ke beech ye growth 14.2% saalana ho gayi. JLL ke policy-driven scenario me, 2030 tak supply 25,000 units se badh ke 74,000 units tak jaa sakti hai, jiske liye lagbhag 7.7 billion dollars ka investment chahiye hoga.

 Iss growth ke peeche policy bhi ek factor hai. Maharashtra ne apni Housing Policy 2025 me senior citizen housing ko ek alag category banaya hai, aur aise ghar khareedne pe stamp duty sirf 1,000 rupaye kar di hai. Haryana ne retirement housing ke liye FAR 2.25 se badha ke 3 kar diya hai, yaani developer usi zameen pe zada built-up area bana sakta hai. Shayad isliye Gurugram aaj Bharat ka sabse hot senior living market ban gaya hai.

 Lekin ek gap abhi bhi bohot bada hai. Assisted living. Yaani woh facilities jahan buzurgon ko roz ki madad aur medical care milti hai. JLL ke hisaab se Bharat me abhi aise sirf 2,100 beds hain. Aur 2030 tak lagbhag 11,000 beds ki zaroorat hogi. Ye point yaad rakhna doston, ispe ham wapas aayenge.

## Part 2: Real Estate Companies Senior Living Me Kaise Khel Rahi Hain

### 2.1 Framework: 4 layers, 2 tarah ka paisa

Doston, senior living ko samajhne ka sabse aasaan tareeka hai hotel business. Jaise hotel me alag alag layers hoti hain, waise hi senior living me bhi 4 layers hain.

Pehli, zameen aur construction. Doosri, ownership, yaani ghar becha jayega ya rent pe diya jayega. Teesri, community operations, yaani maintenance, khana, activities, security. Aur chauthi, care: doctor, nurse, emergency response, physiotherapy.

Ab sabse important baat. In layers me paisa do alag tarah se aata hai. Pehli do layers me paisa ek baar aata hai, jab ghar bikta hai. Aakhri do layers me paisa har mahine aata hai, jab tak buzurg wahan rehte hain.

Toh iss part me hamara ek hi sawaal hai. Aaj companies kaunsi layer se kama rahi hain? Aur long term me asli value kahan banegi?

Har company decide karti hai ki kaunsi layers voh khud rakhegi aur kaunsi partner ko degi. Iss hisaab se Bharat me abhi teen models dikh rahe hain.

### 2.2 Model 1: Sab kuch khud karo

Pehla model hai developer-operator. Yaani zameen kharido, ghar banao, becho, aur fir community ko khud chalao. Pehli teen layers company ke paas.

Ashiana Housing iska sabse bada example hai. Pune ki Paranjape Schemes bhi apne Athashri brand ke saath 16 senior communities bana chuki hai.

Ashiana ke numbers dekho. FY26 me company ne senior living me 570 crores ki bikri ki. Ye ab tak ki sabse zada senior living sales thi, aur FY25 ke 383 crores se lagbhag 49% zada. 5 saal pehle senior living Ashiana ke business ka lagbhag 10% tha. Aaj ye 23% hai. Company ke whole-time director ke hisaab se agle 2-3 saal me ye mix "decisively" senior living ki taraf shift hoga. Aur company ne Pune me apni ab tak ki sabse badi zameen bhi senior living ke liye khareedi hai.

Yaani Ashiana ke liye senior living ab side project nahi raha. Ye core business ban raha hai.

### 2.3 Model 2: Developer banata hai, operator chalata hai

Doosra model partnership ka hai. Zameen aur construction developer karta hai, ghar bhi wahi bechta hai. Aur community chalane aur care dene ka kaam ek specialised senior care company karti hai.

Iska sabse clear example Max Group hai. Max Group ke andar do alag listed companies hain. Max Estates, jo ghar banati aur bechti hai. Aur Max India, jiski subsidiary Antara Senior Living in towers ko manage karti hai.

Ab operator ki economics dekho, kyuki yahi iss model ki khoobsurati hai. Max India ke June 2026 investor presentation ke hisaab se Antara ko project pe 8-10% project management fee milti hai. Steady state pe maintenance income pe 20-25% EBITDA margin. Aur pre-sale 95% se zada, yaani zadatar ghar construction ke dauraan hi bik jaate hain, aur customer ka paisa hi construction fund karta hai.

Aur ek aur revenue stream hai, resale fee. Antara ke Dehradun project me FY26 me 5 ghar resale hue, aur company ne inse lagbhag 1.71 crores ki fee kamayi. Yaani operator ko ek baar nahi, ghar jab jab haath badalta hai, tab tab kamai hoti hai.

Socho, operator ne zameen me ek rupaya nahi lagaya, phir bhi use teen jagah se paisa mil raha hai: fee, maintenance, aur resale. Isiliye ye asset-light model hai.

Aur ye model ab doosre players bhi apna rahe hain. Brigade ne Primus Senior Living ke saath 3 projects announce kiye hain, 600 se zada units, lagbhag 750 crores ka gross development value. Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities Bengaluru me Embassy aur Gardencity, Hyderabad me Prathima aur Pune me Cybercity jaise local developers ke saath kaam kar rahi hai. Serene ke 13 projects me lagbhag 2,600 units under construction hain, 70 lakh se 1.5 crore ki range me.

### 2.4 Model 3: Ghar nahi, care becho

Teesra model sabse alag hai. Yahan ghar becha hi nahi jaata. Buzurg monthly fee de kar rehte hain, aur unhe roz ki madad aur medical care milti hai. Yaani seedha chauthi layer, care.

Serene Communities ne Bengaluru ke Electronic City me 104 beds ki assisted living facility shuru ki hai. Wahan medical care ke saath ek 1BHK ka rent 50,000 se 75,000 rupaye mahina hai, aur medical part KITES Senior Care sambhalti hai. Antara ke paas bhi 4 shehron me 485 care home beds hain.

Aur yaad hai Part 1 me maine kaha tha ki assisted living pe wapas aayenge? Bharat me abhi sirf 2,100 assisted living beds hain, aur 2030 tak lagbhag 11,000 ki zaroorat hogi. Yaani sabse bada gap isi model me hai.

### 2.5 Ek product, do alag market

Ab models chhod ke price dekhte hain, kyuki yahan ek aur kahani hai.

Ashiana ne FY26 me 569 senior units bechi, average lagbhag 1 crore ki. Per square foot ye lagbhag 7,700 rupaye banta hai.

Max Estates ke Estate 360 me 292 senior units July 2025 tak, yaani 11 mahine me, poore bik gaye. Total sales lagbhag 1,530 crores, average lagbhag 20,000 rupaye per square foot, aur ek ghar lagbhag 5 crore ka. December 2025 me Estate 361 launch hua, lagbhag 22,000 rupaye per square foot pe, aur March 2026 tak phase 1 ke 180 me se 127 units bik chuke the.

Aur ab DLF aa raha hai. Gurugram ke Sector 63 me lagbhag 5 lakh square feet ka project, jisse company ko lagbhag 2,000 crores ki sales ki ummeed hai. Simple calculation karo toh ye lagbhag 40,000 rupaye per square foot banta hai. Haan, August 2026 tak ye launch regulatory approvals ki wajah se atka hua tha.

7,700. 20,000. 40,000. Ek hi product category, lekin 5 guna ka fark.

Matlab senior living me do bilkul alag market ban rahe hain. Ek mid-market, Tier 2 aur peripheral shehron me. Aur ek luxury market, metros ke andar, hospitals aur bachchon ke paas. Luxury me bade developers intergenerational communities bhi bana rahe hain, yaani ek hi campus me normal towers bhi aur senior towers bhi, taaki maa-baap aur bachche paas rahein, lekin alag ghar me. Toh luxury buyer sirf ghar ke paise nahi de raha. Voh location aur proximity ke paise de raha hai.

### 2.6 Scorecard: paisa kahan ban raha hai, kahan nahi

Ab wapas apne sawaal pe aate hain. Kaunsi layer kaam kar rahi hai?

Ghar bechna, yaani pehli do layers, saaf kaam kar raha hai. Max ke 292 ghar 11 mahine me bik gaye. Pre-sales 95% se zada. Ashiana ki senior sales ek saal me 49% badh gayi. Developers ke liye ye ek naya premium product ban gaya hai.

Lekin care, yaani chauthi layer, abhi struggle kar rahi hai. Antara ke care homes ka revenue Q4 FY26 me lagbhag 1.6 guna badha, lekin average occupancy sirf 27% thi. Kuch beds abhi abhi add hue hain, isliye occupancy temporarily kam dikh rahi hai. Lekin isi wajah se Max India FY26 me consolidated level pe abhi bhi EBITDA loss me hai.

Aisa kyu? Max India ke presentation me hi iska jawab hai: "kids pay, parents use." Paisa bachche dete hain, lekin rehna maa-baap ko hai. Aur maa-baap ko apna ghar chhod ke care home jaane ke liye convince karna aaj bhi mushkil hai. Isliye log ghar khareedna prefer karte hain, rent pe rehna nahi.

Lekin JLL ka maanna hai ki agle 3-5 saal me rental aur service-led models outright sale ko overtake kar sakte hain. Agar aisa hua, toh ye sector bhi hotels ki tarah development se operations ki taraf shift hoga. Aur tab jeet usi ki hogi jo care ko profitably deliver kar sake, ghar bechne wale ki nahi.

 ## Part 3: Real Estate Alone Won't Be Enough

 Doston, ek simple calculation karte hain. Agar JLL ka sabse optimistic scenario bhi sach ho jaaye, aur 2030 tak 74,000 senior living units ban jaayein, toh bhi ye 21 lakh affluent households ka sirf 3.5% hoga. Yaani Bharat ke 95% se zada buzurg apne hi ghar me budhe honge. Toh elder care ka bada market ghar ke andar hai, community ke andar nahi.

 Isliye real estate ke bahar bhi kai opportunities ban rahe hain.

 Pehla, home healthcare. NITI Aayog ke hisaab se Bharat ka home healthcare market 2020 me lagbhag 50,840 crores ka tha, aur 2027 tak 1.74 lakh crores tak jaa sakta hai.

 Doosra, insurance. April 2024 se IRDAI ne health insurance khareedne ki 65 saal ki age limit hata di hai. Aur sarkaar ne Ayushman Bharat ke tehet 70 saal se upar ke sabhi buzurgon ko 5 lakh tak ka cover dena shuru kiya hai. Lekin February 2025 tak lagbhag 6 crore eligible buzurgon me se sirf 47 lakh ne enrol kiya tha. Yaani awareness abhi bhi ek badi problem hai.

 Teesra, products aur medication. Max India ke hisaab se urban Bharat ke lagbhag 1.5 crore seniors chronic bimariyon ke self-care pe saalana lagbhag 40,000 crores kharch karte hain. Max India ka AGEasy brand, jo walkers, BP monitors aur joint pain jaise products online bechta hai, iss segment pe 47% gross margin kama raha hai.

 Aur chautha, startups. Age Care Labs, jo Emoha aur Epoch Elder Care chalati hai, ne July 2026 me 85 crores raise kiye. Emoha ghar pe emergency response, health monitoring aur hospital accompaniment jaise services deti hai, aur Epoch dementia aur long-term care me hai. Disclosure, iss round me Zerodha ka investment arm Rainmatter bhi investor hai. KITES Senior Care ne Ranjan Pai ke family office se lagbhag 45 crores raise kiye the, aur apne beds ko 340 se 1,000 tak le jaana chahti hai. Khyaal jaisi startups seniors ke liye community aur engagement app bana rahi hain.

 All in all doston, Bharat ki elder care economy abhi shuruaati daur me hai. Demographics clear hain. Buzurg badh rahe hain, families chhoti ho rahi hain. Real estate companies ne pehla bada move kar diya hai, aur ghar bik bhi rahe hain. Lekin iss sector ka asli test care hoga. Kaun si company buzurgon ko achi care de kar profitably scale kar paati hai, wahi long term me jeetegi. Aur ek risk bhi hai. Agar zadatar developers sirf luxury senior homes pe focus karte rahe, toh supply ek chhote se affluent segment me hi atak sakti hai.
