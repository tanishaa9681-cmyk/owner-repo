Pichle do saal mein Bharat ke har bade developer ne senior living me kuch na kuch move announce kiya hai.

DLF, market cap ke hisaab se India ki sabse badi listed real estate company hai. 2026 mein DLF ne Gurugram mein apna pehla senior living project, The Aureva, launch kiya hai. Company iss project se 2,000 crore ka sales target kar rahi thi. Iss project mein 45 floors ka ek tower involved hai, jisme 4 BHK ke 172 flats hain, jinka average ticket size 11.5 crore hai. Launch ke 48 ghante ke andar company ko 406 confirmed expressions of interest mile, yaani har flat ke liye lagbhag 2.4 khareedar. Aur as of October 2026, company ke saare flats book ho gaye, lagbhag 1,985 crore mein.

Ashiana Housing, ek listed real estate company hai jo 1979 se ghar bana rahi hai, aur Bharat mein senior living ki pioneer maani jaati hai. Company ne apna pehla senior living project, Ashiana Utsav, 2007 mein Bhiwadi mein launch kiya tha. Yaani jab baaki developers ne iss segment ke baare mein socha bhi nahi tha, tab se Ashiana yahan hai.

Company ke portfolio mein senior living ka contribution badhta ja raha hai. Paanch saal pehle company ki senior living bookings lagbhag 100 crore thi. FY26 me ye 570 crore ho gayi, aur sirf ek saal me FY25 ke 383 crore se lagbhag 49% badhi. FY21 ke aas paas company ke business mix me senior living ka share 10% tha. FY26 mein ye badh ke 23% ho gaya hai. Whole-time director Varun Gupta ke hisaab se agle 2-3 saal me mix "decisively" senior living ki taraf shift hoga. Company senior living ko saalana 25% badhane ka target rakh rahi hai, aur unke shabdon mein, company ke paas aage ke liye jo 50 lakh square feet zameen hai, "the entire 5 million square feet is senior living."

Company ne Pune mein senior living ke liye 28.55 acre ki zameen khareedi hai. Ye unki abtak ki sabse badi land deal hai. Iss project mein lagbhag 20 lakh square feet develop hoga, jisse company ko 1,800 se 2,000 crore ki revenue ki ummeed hai, aur ye agle financial year ke doosre half mein launch ho sakta hai.

Aur yahan ek financial angle bhi hai. Company ke CFO Vikas Dugal ke hisaab se, jab senior living ki sales lagbhag 1,500 crore ke "critical mass" tak pahunchegi, tab operating leverage poori tarah kaam karegi, aur margins aur return on equity dono behtar honge. Yaani Ashiana ke liye senior living sirf growth nahi, profitability ka bhi raasta hai.

Iske alawa, Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities 6 shehron me lagbhag 3,000 crore invest kar rahi hai, aur 13 projects me lagbhag 2,600 ghar bana rahi hai, 70 lakh se 1.5 crore ki range me. Brigade Group ne Primus Senior Living ke saath 3 projects announce kiye hain, 600 se zada ghar, lagbhag 750 crore ka gross development value. Godrej Properties ne North Bengaluru me Manasum Senior Living ke saath 'Banyan' project shuru kiya hai. Hiranandani Group Chennai ke paas 400 ghar ka 'Elements' project la raha hai. Aur Pune ki Paranjape Schemes apne Athashri brand ke saath pehle se 16 senior communities chala rahi hai.

DLF, Max Estates, Godrej Properties aur Hiranandani jaisi companies metro cities ke luxury customers ko target kar rahi hain. Aur Ashiana, Serene Communities aur Paranjape ka Athashri jaise players mid-market aur Tier 2 customers ko target kar rahe hain. Fark kitna bada hai, iska andaaza ticket size se lagao. Ashiana ka ek ghar lagbhag 1 crore ka hai, Max Estates ka lagbhag 5 crore, aur DLF ka 11.5 crore. Ek hi category mein 10 guna se zada ka fark.

Aur dosto, companies sirf properties build karne tak seemit nahi hain. Iske alawa, companies ab inn properties mein senior care se associated services par bhi focus kar rahi hain. Kyuki dosto, sirf buildings ban jaane se senior care ensure nahi ho jata hai. Seniors ki comfortable living ke liye 24 ghante emergency response, nurse aur doctor tak turant pahunch, unki sehat ke hisaab se khana, aur akelepan se bachne ke liye ek community bhi zaroori hai.

### Ye shift senior care ke liye zaroori kyu hai

Iska simple reason ye hai ki senior living mein building asli product nahi hai. Asli product service hai. Ek family jab 5 crore ka senior home khareedti hai, toh voh teen bedroom ke paise nahi de rahi. Voh paise de rahi hai iss bharose ke, ki raat 2 baje emergency hui toh nurse aayegi, aur ambulance "golden hour" ke andar bade hospital tak pahunchegi. Colliers ki report ye seedha kehti hai, senior living "not a build-and-sell product rather it is an operating business."

Doosra, developer ki kamai ghar ke handover pe khatam ho jaati hai. Lekin buzurg ki care ki zaroorat uske baad agle 15-20 saal tak badhti hai. Agar koi community ko achhe se chalaye hi nahi, toh wahi ghar kuch saal baad bekaar ho jaata hai. Aur senior home sirf doosre senior ko hi resale ho sakta hai, aur woh bhi tab, jab community achhe se chal rahi ho.

Teesra, iss market mein bharosa sab kuch hai. Colliers ke hisaab se ek trusted operator ke 30-50% naye residents referrals se aate hain: purane residents, doctors aur nurses ke through. Ye reputation building se nahi, service se banti hai.

Aur chautha, sabse bada gap bhi yahin hai. Part 1 mein humne dekha tha ki Bharat mein abhi sirf lagbhag 2,100 assisted living beds hain, jabki 2030 tak lagbhag 11,000 ki zaroorat hogi. Ye gap sirf ghar bana ke nahi bharega.

### Doosre deshon mein kya hua

Jin deshon mein senior living mature ho chuka hai, wahan ye shift bahut pehle ho chuka hai.

America mein "active adult" rental communities ek alag asset class ban gayi hain. Log ghar khareedte nahi, rent pe rehte hain, aur company services se kamati hai. UK mein pension funds iss business ke maalik ban gaye. Late 2025 mein NatWest Pension Trustees ne Legal & General se Inspired Villages ka 100% hissa khareed liya, ek chalte hue senior living business ko lambe samay tak hold karne ke liye. China mein insurance companies hi developer ban gayi. Taikang Insurance mein aap ek whole-life policy khareedte ho, aur retirement pe cash ke bajaye aapko ya aapke maa-baap ko Taikang ki premium retirement community mein guaranteed jagah milti hai. Aur Japan ne ise sarkari long-term care insurance ke through poore system ka hissa bana diya.

Har jagah pattern ek hi hai. Building ka maalik ek lamba sochne wala investor hota hai, jaise pension fund, insurer ya REIT. Aur use chalata hai ek specialist operator. Bilkul hotels ki tarah, jahan building kisi ki hoti hai aur brand kisi aur ka. Colliers ke hisaab se Bharat abhi bhi developer-led phase mein hai, aur agle 5 saal mein developer-operator partnerships mainstream ho jayengi.

### Real estate companies ke liye ye kyu sense banata hai

Ab sawaal ye hai ki agar ghar itni tezi se bik hi rahe hain, toh developer services ke jhanjhat mein kyu pade? Iska jawab numbers mein hai.

Pehla, ek baar ki kamai ke upar recurring kamai. Max Group ka example dekho. Group ki ek company, Max Estates, ghar banati aur bechti hai. Aur doosri, Max India ki subsidiary Antara, unhe chalati hai. Max India ke June 2026 investor presentation ke hisaab se Antara ko project value ka 8-10% project management fee milti hai. Q4 FY26 earnings call mein management ne bataya ki Estate 360 se lagbhag 130 crore aur Estate 361 se lagbhag 200 crore ki management fee expected hai. Aur CFO Ajay Agarwal ke hisaab se iss pe direct variable cost sirf lagbhag 15% hai. Iske upar maintenance income pe steady state mein 20-25% EBITDA margin, aur jab bhi ghar resale ho, tab resale fee. Antara ke Dehradun project mein FY26 mein 5 ghar resale hue, aur company ne inse lagbhag 1.71 crore kamaye. Aur ye sab, zameen mein ek rupaya lagaye bina.

Doosra, ek stabilised community apne aap mein profitable hai. Antara ki sabse purani Dehradun community ne FY26 mein lagbhag 6.65 crore ka revenue kiya, aur company ke hisaab se voh cash aur profit surplus mein hai.

Teesra, services se ghar jaldi aur mehenge bikte hain. Max India ke hisaab se aise projects mein 95% se zada ghar construction ke dauraan hi bik jaate hain. Yaani customer ka advance hi building banata hai. Aur ek trusted operator ka brand developer ko premium pricing deta hai.

Aur chautha, ye business ko scale deta hai. Ashiana ke CFO ki baat yaad karo. Senior living jab critical mass pe pahunchegi, tab operating leverage se margins aur ROE dono badhenge.

### Pure capex ke alawa companies kis tarah ki deals kar rahi hain

Isiliye ab companies sirf zameen aur building pe paisa nahi laga rahi. Chaar tarah ki deals dikh rahi hain.

**Pehli, developer aur operator ki partnership.** Developer ghar banata hai, aur specialist operator community chalata hai. Brigade ne Primus ke saath, Godrej Properties ne Manasum ke saath, aur Embassy ne Serene ke saath yahi kiya hai. Primus akele Brigade, Wadhwa, Dosti Realty, Srijan aur Naiknavare jaise developers ke saath 6 shehron mein 9 projects pe kaam kar rahi hai.

**Doosri, institutional platforms.** Primus ne HDFC Capital ke saath 2,000 crore ka senior living platform banaya hai. Ye iss sector mein bade institutional paise ke sabse clear signals mein se ek hai. Investors ab ek project mein nahi, poore operating platform mein paisa laga rahe hain.

**Teesri, care partnerships.** Developers care wali layer specialists ko de rahe hain. Ashiana assisted living aur dementia care ke liye Epoch Elder Care ke saath kaam karti hai. Serene ne Bengaluru ke Electronic City mein 104 beds ki assisted living facility shuru ki hai, jiska medical part KITES Senior Care sambhalti hai. Aur iske saath bade hospitals ke saath tie-ups ho rahe hain, taaki emergency mein "golden hour" ke andar treatment mile.

**Chauthi, ghar bechne ke alawa naye models.** Serene ki assisted living facility mein ghar becha nahi jaata. Medical care ke saath ek 1BHK ka rent 50,000 se 75,000 rupaye mahina hai. Colliers ke hisaab se Prestige Group bhi rent model pe hospitality-led senior living mein aane ki planning kar raha hai, aur iske liye alag leadership team hire ki hai. Aur aage deferred ownership jaise models bhi aa sakte hain, jahan buzurg ghar ki value ka 50-70% refundable deposit dete hain, zindagi bhar rehte hain, aur baad mein deposit unke heirs ko wapas milta hai.

### Lekin ye raasta aasaan nahi hai

Ab doosra pehlu bhi dekhna zaroori hai. Services ka business dheere paisa banata hai. Colliers ke hisaab se ek senior living project ko stable occupancy tak pahunchne mein 4 se 6 saal lagte hain.

Aur care, jo sabse zaroori layer hai, abhi sabse mushkil bhi hai. Max India ke care homes ka revenue Q4 FY26 mein lagbhag 11.4 crore raha, saal dar saal 1.6 guna. Lekin 485 beds mein average occupancy sirf 27% thi. Isi wajah se Max India ka EBITDA loss FY25 ke lagbhag 99 crore se ghat ke FY26 mein lagbhag 83 crore hua. Direction sahi hai, lekin company abhi bhi loss mein hai. Management ke hisaab se scale pe care homes ka EBITDA margin 18-20% aur return on capital lagbhag 25-26% ho sakta hai. Lekin abhi ye promise hai, result nahi.

Iske peeche ek cultural wajah bhi hai. Max India ke presentation mein likha hai, "kids pay, parents use." Paisa bachche dete hain, lekin rehna maa-baap ko hai. Aur maa-baap ko apna ghar chhod ke shift hone ke liye manana aaj bhi mushkil hai.

Toh picture kuch aisi hai dosto. Ghar bechne wala business aaj profit kama raha hai. Ghar chalane wala business, khaaskar care, abhi loss mein hai, lekin long-term value wahin hai. JLL ke hisaab se agle 3-5 saal mein rental aur service-led models outright sale ko overtake kar sakte hain. Aur jo company care ko profitably deliver kar payegi, wahi iss sector mein long term mein jeetegi.
