Senior care industry mein real-estate companies majorly do approaches follow kar rahi hain. Pehla hai pure play real-estate mode jaha companies ka role sirf real estate projects build karne tak hi seemit hai. Aur doosra hai ek integrated approach. Jaha companies real estate projects ke sath residents ke health care, caregiving aur community needs par bhi focus kar rahi hain. Chaliye inhe detail mein samajhte hain.

## Pure-Play Real Estate Approach

DLF, market cap ke hisaab se India ki sabse badi real estate company hai. 2026 mein DLF ne Gurugram mein apna pehla senior living project, The Aureva, launch kiya hai. Company iss project se 2,000 crore ka sales target kar rahi thi. Iss project mein 45 floors ka ek tower involved hai, jisme 4 BHK ke 172 flats hai, jinka average ticket size 11.5 crore hai. As of October 2026, company ke saare flats book ho gaye.

Ashiana Housing, kaafi time se real-estate segment mein active hai. Company ne apna pehla senior living project 2007 mein launch kiya tha. Company ke portfolio mein senior living ka contribution badhta ja raha hai. Paanch saal pehle company ki senior living bookings lagbhag 100 crore thi. FY26 me ye 570 crore ho gayi, aur sirf ek saal me FY25 ke 383 crore se lagbhag 49% badhi. FY21 mein company ke business mix me senior living ka share 10% tha. FY26 mein ye badh ke 23% ho gaya hai. Whole-time director ke hisaab se agle 2-3 saal me mix "decisively" senior living ki taraf shift hoga. Company ka target hai ki unka senior care segment 25% annually grow kare.

Company ne Pune mein senior living ke liye 28.55 acre ki zameen khareedi hai. Ye unki abtak ki sabse badi land deal hai. Iss project ke through company 1,800 se 2,000 crore ka revenue expect kar rahi hai.

Iske alawa Pune ki Paranjape Schemes apne Athashri brand ke saath 16 senior communities bana chuki hai, jahan 3,000 se zada buzurg rehte hain. Hyderabad mein Saket Engineers ka Saket Pranamam bhi isi model pe chalta hai. Aur Hiranandani Group Chennai ke paas Oragadam mein 400 ghar ka premium 'Elements' project la raha hai.

Ashiana, Paranjape ka Athashri aur Saket Pranamam jaisi companies mid-market aur Tier 2 customers ko target kar rahi hain, jahan ek ghar lagbhag 1 crore ke aas paas hai. Aur DLF aur Hiranandani jaisi companies metro cities ke premium aur luxury end ko target kar rahi hain, jahan DLF ka ek ghar 11.5 crore ka hai.

## Integrated Approach

Chaliye ab real-estate companies ke doosre approach ko samajhte hain. Dosto, sirf buildings ban jaane se senior care ensure nahi ho jata hai. Seniors ki comfortable living ke liye caregiver availability, social connection aur healthcare access bhi equally important hain.

Bharat mein senior care abhi tak service-led segment nahi bana hai. Abhi, yaha zyadatar property development mein focus kiya ja raha tha. Jabki, China, Japan, US aur UK jaise markets mein properties ke sath healthcare, insurance, financial help aur community building jaise services bhi integrated hain.

Jaise China mein Taikang jaisi insurance company aapko ek life insurance policy bechti hai, aur retirement pe cash ke bajaye aapko apni retirement community mein guaranteed jagah deti hai. UK mein pension funds poore senior living business khareed rahe hain. Late 2025 mein NatWest Pension Trustees ne Inspired Villages ka 100% hissa khareed liya. Aur Japan mein sarkari long-term care insurance ke through care ko ghar se 30 minute ke andar laaya gaya hai. Yaani in markets mein building ek platform hai, aur asli business uske upar chalne wali services hain.

Bharat mein bhi ab yahi shift shuru ho raha hai, aur iski teen wajah hain.

Pehli, customer building nahi, bharosa khareed raha hai. Ek family jab 5 crore ka senior home leti hai, toh voh iss bharose ke paise de rahi hai ki raat 2 baje emergency hui toh nurse aayegi, aur ambulance "golden hour" ke andar hospital tak pahunchegi. Colliers ke hisaab se ek trusted operator ke 30-50% naye residents referrals se hi aate hain.

Doosri, developer ki kamai handover pe khatam ho jaati hai, lekin buzurg ki zaroorat agle 15-20 saal chalti hai. Jo community chalata hai, usse har mahine kamai hoti hai. Max India ke hisaab se uski subsidiary Antara ko project value ka 8-10% management fee milti hai, aur maintenance pe 20-25% EBITDA margin.

Teesri, developer ke paas zameen aur construction ki expertise hai, care ki nahi. Isiliye zyadatar developers ye kaam khud karne ke bajaye specialist operators ke saath partnership kar rahe hain. Colliers ke hisaab se agle 5 saal mein developer-operator partnerships iss sector mein mainstream ho jayengi.

Aur ye partnerships bhi ek jaisi nahi hain. Operator kis cheez pe focus karta hai, uske hisaab se inhe teen hisson mein dekh sakte hain.

### 1. Lifestyle aur hospitality-led

Yahan focus hai ek 5-star hotel jaisa experience: dining, wellness, activities, aur saath mein preventive healthcare. Ye model zyadatar affluent seniors aur NRI families ko target karta hai.

Iska sabse bada example Max Group hai. Max Estates ghar banati aur bechti hai, aur Max India ki subsidiary Antara Senior Living unhe chalati hai. Max Estates ne Gurugram ke Estate 360 mein 292 senior homes 11 mahine mein bech diye, lagbhag 1,530 crore mein, yaani ek ghar lagbhag 5 crore ka. December 2025 mein launch hue Estate 361 mein March 2026 tak phase 1 ke 180 mein se 127 ghar bik chuke the. Aur management ke hisaab se Antara ko Estate 360 se lagbhag 130 crore aur Estate 361 se lagbhag 200 crore ki management fee milne ki ummeed hai, jispe direct variable cost sirf lagbhag 15% hai.

Godrej Properties ne bhi khud operator banne ke bajaye Manasum Senior Living ke saath North Bengaluru mein 'Banyan' naam ki luxury community shuru ki hai. Manasum Goa mein Prescon Group ke saath bhi kaam karti hai. Aur Gurugram mein J K Urbanscapes ne Advait Living ke saath premium retirement housing banayi hai.

### 2. Healthcare-led

Doosra model clinical care pe focus karta hai: assisted living, dementia care, post-hospitalisation recovery aur skilled nursing. Yahan developer building deta hai, aur ek healthcare-led operator care.

Bengaluru ki Embassy Group, Chennai ki TVS Emerald aur Kerala ki Asset Homes, ye teeno Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities ke saath partner hain. Serene lagbhag 3,000 crore invest kar rahi hai, 13 projects mein lagbhag 2,600 ghar bana rahi hai, aur abhi 10 communities mein lagbhag 1,700 ghar manage karti hai. Iska focus continuum of care pe hai, yaani independent living se lekar assisted living aur memory care tak, ek hi jagah.

Ashiana Housing, jo khud pure-play model pe chalti hai, wo bhi assisted living aur dementia care ke liye Epoch Elder Care ke saath partner karti hai. Yaani sabse bada pure-play developer bhi care ka kaam specialist ko de raha hai. Chennai mein Ramaniyam Real Estates aur VNCT Global ne Athulya Senior Care ke saath, aur Bengaluru mein Ozone Group aur Mysore mein Tapovan Projects ne Covai Care ke saath partnership ki hai.

### 3. Hybrid aur multi-generational

Teesra model hospitality aur healthcare dono ko milata hai, aur saath mein ek naya twist deta hai: multi-generational living. Yahan maa-baap ek serviced senior apartment mein rehte hain, aur bachche bagal wale normal tower mein. Colliers iss model ko Bharat ke liye "sweet spot" kehti hai, kyunki ye bachchon ka guilt aur maa-baap ka akelapan, dono ek saath address karta hai.

Iska sabse bada naam Primus Senior Living hai, jo developer aur operator dono hai. Brigade Group ne Primus ke saath 3 projects announce kiye hain, 600 se zada ghar, lagbhag 750 crore ka gross development value. Iske alawa Primus Wadhwa Group, Dosti Realty, Srijan Realty, Naiknavare Developers aur Gopalan Enterprises ke saath 6 shehron mein 9 projects pe kaam kar rahi hai. Aur Primus ne HDFC Capital ke saath 2,000 crore ka senior living platform bhi banaya hai, jo iss sector mein institutional paise ke sabse bade signals mein se ek hai.

Integrated approach mein bhi target market alag alag hai. Max Group ka Antara, Godrej-Manasum aur Advait jaise lifestyle-led players premium aur luxury end ko target kar rahe hain. Aur Serene, Primus aur Covai Care jaise players mid-market ko, jahan ek ghar lagbhag 70 lakh se 1.5 crore ka hai.

### Lekin ye raasta aasaan nahi hai

Ab doosra pehlu bhi dekhna zaroori hai. Services ka business dheere paisa banata hai. Colliers ke hisaab se ek senior living project ko stable occupancy tak pahunchne mein 4 se 6 saal lagte hain.

Aur care, jo sabse zaroori layer hai, abhi sabse mushkil bhi hai. Max India ke care homes ka revenue Q4 FY26 mein lagbhag 11.4 crore raha, saal dar saal 1.6 guna. Lekin 485 beds mein average occupancy sirf 27% thi. Isi wajah se Max India ka EBITDA loss FY25 ke lagbhag 99 crore se ghat ke FY26 mein lagbhag 83 crore hua. Direction sahi hai, lekin company abhi bhi loss mein hai. Management ke hisaab se scale pe care homes ka EBITDA margin 18-20% aur return on capital lagbhag 25-26% ho sakta hai. Lekin abhi ye promise hai, result nahi.

Iske peeche ek cultural wajah bhi hai. Max India ke presentation mein likha hai, "kids pay, parents use." Paisa bachche dete hain, lekin rehna maa-baap ko hai. Aur maa-baap ko apna ghar chhod ke shift hone ke liye manana aaj bhi mushkil hai.

Toh picture kuch aisi hai dosto. Ghar bechne wala business aaj profit kama raha hai. Ghar chalane wala business, khaaskar care, abhi loss mein hai, lekin long-term value wahin hai. JLL ke hisaab se agle 3-5 saal mein rental aur service-led models outright sale ko overtake kar sakte hain. Aur jo company care ko profitably deliver kar payegi, wahi iss sector mein long term mein jeetegi.
