Doston, Bharat ke real estate sector me ek naya segment chupchaap bada ho raha hai. Senior living. Yaani buzurgon ke liye specially design kiye gaye ghar. Aur iss segment me kaafi announcements hue hain. Desh ki sabse badi listed real estate company DLF Gurugram me apna pehla senior living project launch karne wali hai, jisse company lagbhag 2,000 crores kamane ki ummeed kar rahi hai. Max Estates ne Gurugram me 292 senior homes launch kiye the, aur saare ke saare 11 mahine me bik gaye. Average ticket size? Lagbhag 5 crore rupaye. Brigade ne Primus Senior Living ke saath 750 crores ke projects announce kiye hain. Aur Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities 6 shehron me 3,000 crores invest kar rahi hai.

Lekin yahan ek interesting baat hai. JLL aur Association of Senior Living India ki report ke hisaab se, Bharat me lagbhag 17 lakh aise senior households hain jo shehron me rehte hain aur financially independent hain. Lekin unke liye organised senior living units sirf 25,050 hain. Yaani penetration sirf 1.5%. America me ye number 6-7% hai, aur New Zealand me 14-15%.

 Doston, welcome to Markets by Zerodha. Mai hun Shantanu Jain, aur aaj ham Bharat ki elder care economy ko samjhenge. Pehle dekhenge ki ye market kitna bada hai aur kyu badh raha hai. Fir samjhenge ki real estate companies iss sector me kis strategy se aa rahi hain, aur kiska model kaam kar raha hai. Aur last me dekhenge ki sirf ghar banana kaafi kyu nahi hai. Shuru karte hain.

 ## Part 1: India's Ageing Economy

 UNFPA ki India Ageing Report ke hisaab se, 2022 me Bharat me 60 saal se upar ke lagbhag 14.9 crore log the. Yaani population ka 10.5%. 2050 tak ye number lagbhag dugna ho kar 34.7 crore ho jayega, yaani har 5 me se 1 Bharatiya senior citizen hoga. Aur report ke hisaab se, 2046 ke aas paas Bharat me buzurgon ki sankhya 0 se 14 saal ke bachchon se zada ho jayegi. Ek aur interesting data point. JLL ke hisaab se 75 saal se upar ki population har saal 7.8% badh rahi hai. Aur yahi woh age group hai jise sabse zada care ki zaroorat hoti hai.

 Lekin sirf buzurgon ki sankhya badhna kaafi nahi hai. Asli badlav family structure me aa raha hai. Max India ke investor presentation ke hisaab se, aaj 70% families nuclear hain. Bachche padhai aur naukri ke liye doosre shehron ya videsh chale jaate hain. Toh joint family wala jo default care system tha, voh tootta jaa raha hai.

 Ab yahan doston ek important baat samajhni hogi. Bharat ki senior population ek market nahi, balki do market hai. Ek taraf, wahi presentation kehta hai ki 78% buzurgon ke paas koi pension nahi hai, aur sirf 18% ke paas health insurance hai. NITI Aayog ki report ke hisaab se 75% se zada buzurgon ko koi na koi chronic bimari hai. Aur Max India ke data ke hisaab se 24% buzurg rozmarra ke kaam khud se nahi kar paate. Ye woh market hai jahan zaroorat sabse zada hai, lekin paisa kam.

Doosri taraf hai affluent urban senior. Jo retired hai lekin financially independent hai, jiske bachche bahar settled hain, aur jo 1-5 crore ka ghar khareed sakta hai. JLL ke hisaab se aise households 2026 me 17 lakh hain, aur 2030 tak 21 lakh ho jayenge. Abhi zadatar companies issi doosre market ko target kar rahi hain.

 Toh ye market kitna bada hai? NITI Aayog ne poori senior care industry ko lagbhag 7 billion dollars ka estimate kiya tha. Aur JLL-ASLI ki September 2026 ki report ke hisaab se, sirf senior living ka market 2030 tak 10.1 billion dollars ka opportunity ban sakta hai.

 Aur supply bhi ab tezi se badh rahi hai. 2019 se 2023 ke beech organised senior living supply saalana 8.5% badh rahi thi. Lekin 2024 se June 2026 ke beech ye growth 14.2% saalana ho gayi. JLL ke policy-driven scenario me, 2030 tak supply 25,000 units se badh ke 74,000 units tak jaa sakti hai, jiske liye lagbhag 7.7 billion dollars ka investment chahiye hoga.

 Iss growth ke peeche policy bhi ek factor hai. Maharashtra ne apni Housing Policy 2025 me senior citizen housing ko ek alag category banaya hai, aur aise ghar khareedne pe stamp duty sirf 1,000 rupaye kar di hai. Haryana ne retirement housing ke liye FAR 2.25 se badha ke 3 kar diya hai, yaani developer usi zameen pe zada built-up area bana sakta hai. Shayad isliye Gurugram aaj Bharat ka sabse hot senior living market ban gaya hai.

 Lekin ek gap abhi bhi bohot bada hai. Assisted living. Yaani woh facilities jahan buzurgon ko roz ki madad aur medical care milti hai. JLL ke hisaab se Bharat me abhi aise sirf 2,100 beds hain. Aur 2030 tak lagbhag 11,000 beds ki zaroorat hogi. Ye point yaad rakhna doston, ispe ham wapas aayenge.

 ## Part 2: How Real Estate Companies Are Playing Senior Living

 Doston, hotel sector ki tarah senior living business ko bhi 4 layers me samjha jaa sakta hai. Pehla, zameen aur construction. Doosra, ghar ki ownership. Kya ghar becha jayega ya rent pe diya jayega. Teesra, community operations. Yaani maintenance, khana, activities, security. Aur chautha, care. Doctor, nurse, emergency response, physiotherapy.

 Har company decide karti hai ki inme se kaunsi layer voh khud rakhegi aur kaunsi partner ko degi. Aur abhi Bharat me 4 major strategies dikh rahi hain.

Pehli strategy hai poora kaam khud karna. Zameen kharido, ghar banao, becho, aur fir community ko khud chalao. Ashiana Housing iss model ka sabse bada example hai. Pune ki Paranjape Schemes bhi apne Athashri brand ke saath 16 senior communities bana chuki hai.

 Ashiana ke numbers dekho. FY26 me company ne senior living me 570 crores ki bikri ki. Ye company ki ab tak ki sabse zada senior living sales thi, aur FY25 ke 383 crores se lagbhag 49% zada. Company ke whole-time director ke hisaab se, 5 saal pehle senior living Ashiana ke business ka lagbhag 10% tha. Aaj ye 23% hai. Aur agle 2-3 saal me ye mix "decisively" senior living ki taraf shift hoga. Company ne Pune me apni ab tak ki sabse badi zameen bhi senior living project ke liye khareedi hai.

 Lekin Ashiana ke ghar mid-market hain. FY26 me company ne 569 senior units bechi, yaani average ek unit lagbhag 1 crore ki. Per square foot ke hisaab se ye lagbhag 7,700 rupaye banta hai. Ye number yaad rakhna, kyuki doosre players ke saath comparison me ye bohot interesting ho jayega.

 Doosri strategy hai developer aur specialist operator ki partnership. Yaani zameen aur construction developer karega. Aur community chalane aur care dene ka kaam ek specialised senior care company karegi. Operator ke liye ye asset-light model hai, kyuki use zameen me paise nahi lagane padte.

 Iska sabse clear example hai Max Group. Max Group ke andar do alag listed companies hain. Max Estates, jo real estate develop karti hai. Aur Max India, jiski subsidiary Antara Senior Living hai. Max Estates ghar banati aur bechti hai, aur Antara senior living towers ko manage karti hai.

 Max India ke June 2026 investor presentation ke hisaab se, iss model ki typical economics kuch aisi hai. Antara ko project pe 8-10% project management fee milti hai. Steady state pe maintenance income pe 20-25% EBITDA margin. Aur pre-sale rate 95% se zada. Yaani zadatar units construction ke dauraan hi bik jaate hain, aur customer ka paisa hi construction ko fund karta hai.

 Aur ek interesting revenue stream bhi hai. Resale fee. Antara ke Dehradun project me FY26 me 5 ghar resale hue, aur company ne inse lagbhag 1.71 crores ki fee kamayi. Yaani operator ko sirf ek baar nahi, balki ghar jab jab haath badalta hai, tab tab kamai hoti hai.

Ye model ab doosre players bhi apna rahe hain. Brigade ne Primus Senior Living ke saath partnership ki hai. 3 projects, 600 se zada units, aur lagbhag 750 crores ka gross development value. Columbia Pacific ki Serene Communities Bengaluru me Embassy aur Gardencity, Hyderabad me Prathima aur Pune me Cybercity jaise local developers ke saath partner kar rahi hai. Serene ke 13 projects me lagbhag 2,600 units under construction hain, aur ghar 70 lakh se 1.5 crore ki range me hain.

Teesri strategy hai bade developers ka luxury aur intergenerational communities me aana. Intergenerational ka matlab, ek hi bade project me normal residential towers bhi honge aur senior living towers bhi. Taaki bachche aur maa-baap ek hi campus me, lekin alag alag ghar me reh sakein.

Max Estates ka Estate 360 iska example hai. Iske 292 senior living units July 2025 tak poore bik gaye, aur inki total gross sales lagbhag 1,530 crores thi. Average price lagbhag 20,000 rupaye per square foot. Uske baad December 2025 me launch hua Estate 361. Wahan senior homes lagbhag 22,000 rupaye per square foot pe bik rahe hain, aur March 2026 tak phase 1 ke 180 me se 127 units bik chuke the.

Aur ab DLF bhi aa raha hai. DLF Gurugram ke Sector 63 me lagbhag 5 lakh square feet ka senior living project launch karne wala hai, jisse company ko lagbhag 2,000 crores ki sales ki ummeed hai. Simple calculation karo, toh ye lagbhag 40,000 rupaye per square foot banta hai. Haan, August 2026 tak ye launch regulatory approvals ki wajah se atka hua tha.

 Ab ye comparison dekho doston. Ashiana lagbhag 7,700 rupaye per square foot. Max Estates 20,000-22,000. DLF lagbhag 40,000. Yaani senior living me bhi ek nahi, do bilkul alag markets ban rahe hain. Ek mid-market jo Tier 2 aur peripheral shehron me hai. Aur ek luxury market jo metros ke andar hai, hospitals aur bachchon ke paas. Iss table me hamne saare major players ke projects, pricing aur model summarise kiye hain. Aap iska screenshot le sakte ho.

 Aur chauthi strategy hai care-led model. Yaani ghar bechna nahi, balki assisted living aur care homes chalana, jahan buzurg monthly fees de kar rehte hain. Serene Communities ne Bengaluru ke Electronic City me 104 beds ki assisted living facility shuru ki hai, jahan ek 1BHK ka rent medical care ke saath 50,000 se 75,000 rupaye mahina hai. Iska medical part KITES Senior Care sambhalti hai.


Lekin yahi woh jagah hai doston, jahan iss sector ki asli challenge dikhti hai. Antara ke paas 4 shehron me 485 care home beds hain. Revenue tezi se badh raha hai, Q4 FY26 me lagbhag 1.6 guna. Lekin average occupancy sirf 27% thi. Haan, kuch beds abhi abhi add hue hain, isliye occupancy temporarily kam dikh rahi hai. Lekin isi wajah se Max India FY26 me consolidated level pe abhi bhi EBITDA loss me hai.

 Toh picture kuch aisi ban rahi hai. Senior homes bechna kaam kar raha hai. Customers advance me paise de rahe hain, aur developers ke liye ye ek naya premium product ban gaya hai. Lekin recurring care business, jo long term me asli value create karega, voh abhi scale aur profitability dhoond raha hai.

 Iske peeche ek cultural reason bhi hai. Max India ke presentation me hi likha hai ki Bharat me penetration kam rehne ki ek badi wajah ye hai ki "kids pay, parents use." Yaani paisa bachche dete hain, lekin rehna maa-baap ko hai. Aur maa-baap ko ghar chhod ke care home jaane ke liye convince karna aaj bhi mushkil hai. Shayad isliye abhi zadatar log ghar khareedna prefer kar rahe hain, rent pe rehna nahi.

 Lekin JLL ka maanna hai ki agle 3-5 saal me rental aur service-led models outright sale ko overtake kar sakte hain. Agar aisa hua, toh iss sector ka business model hotels ki tarah, development se operations ki taraf shift hoga. Aur tab asli sawaal ye hoga ki kaun si company care ko profitably deliver kar sakti hai. Iss table me hamne alag alag strategies aur unke economics ko summarise kiya hai. Ye bhi reference ke liye rakh lo.

 ## Part 3: Real Estate Alone Won't Be Enough

 Doston, ek simple calculation karte hain. Agar JLL ka sabse optimistic scenario bhi sach ho jaaye, aur 2030 tak 74,000 senior living units ban jaayein, toh bhi ye 21 lakh affluent households ka sirf 3.5% hoga. Yaani Bharat ke 95% se zada buzurg apne hi ghar me budhe honge. Toh elder care ka bada market ghar ke andar hai, community ke andar nahi.

 Isliye real estate ke bahar bhi kai opportunities ban rahe hain.

 Pehla, home healthcare. NITI Aayog ke hisaab se Bharat ka home healthcare market 2020 me lagbhag 50,840 crores ka tha, aur 2027 tak 1.74 lakh crores tak jaa sakta hai.

 Doosra, insurance. April 2024 se IRDAI ne health insurance khareedne ki 65 saal ki age limit hata di hai. Aur sarkaar ne Ayushman Bharat ke tehet 70 saal se upar ke sabhi buzurgon ko 5 lakh tak ka cover dena shuru kiya hai. Lekin February 2025 tak lagbhag 6 crore eligible buzurgon me se sirf 47 lakh ne enrol kiya tha. Yaani awareness abhi bhi ek badi problem hai.

 Teesra, products aur medication. Max India ke hisaab se urban Bharat ke lagbhag 1.5 crore seniors chronic bimariyon ke self-care pe saalana lagbhag 40,000 crores kharch karte hain. Max India ka AGEasy brand, jo walkers, BP monitors aur joint pain jaise products online bechta hai, iss segment pe 47% gross margin kama raha hai.

 Aur chautha, startups. Age Care Labs, jo Emoha aur Epoch Elder Care chalati hai, ne July 2026 me 85 crores raise kiye. Emoha ghar pe emergency response, health monitoring aur hospital accompaniment jaise services deti hai, aur Epoch dementia aur long-term care me hai. Disclosure, iss round me Zerodha ka investment arm Rainmatter bhi investor hai. KITES Senior Care ne Ranjan Pai ke family office se lagbhag 45 crores raise kiye the, aur apne beds ko 340 se 1,000 tak le jaana chahti hai. Khyaal jaisi startups seniors ke liye community aur engagement app bana rahi hain.

 All in all doston, Bharat ki elder care economy abhi shuruaati daur me hai. Demographics clear hain. Buzurg badh rahe hain, families chhoti ho rahi hain. Real estate companies ne pehla bada move kar diya hai, aur ghar bik bhi rahe hain. Lekin iss sector ka asli test care hoga. Kaun si company buzurgon ko achi care de kar profitably scale kar paati hai, wahi long term me jeetegi. Aur ek risk bhi hai. Agar zadatar developers sirf luxury senior homes pe focus karte rahe, toh supply ek chhote se affluent segment me hi atak sakti hai.

 Aapko kya lagta hai? Kya Bharatiya families apne maa-baap ke liye senior living ya care homes ko accept karengi? Ya ghar pe care hi default rahega? Apne thoughts comments me zarur batana. Mai hun Shantanu Jain, milta hun aapse next Saturday.
