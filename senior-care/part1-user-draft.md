Part 1 India’s Ageing Economy
`
Aaj ke din Bharat ki jansankhya ka kul xx% senior citizen ki category mein aata hain. Aur 2050 tak ye number dugna ho kar 34.6 crores ho jaega. Isme bhi 75 saal se upar ki population zada tezi se badhegi. JLL ke hisaab se 75 saal se upar ki population har saal 7.8% badh rahi hai. Yaani, aane waale kuch saalon mein har 5 mein se 1 Bhartiya senior citizen hoga. In fact xx report ke hisaab se, 2046 ke aaspaas Bharat mein buzurgo ki sankhya 0-14 saal ke ke bachchon se zyada ho jaegi.

Aur dosto, iss age group ke logo ki kaafi saari vulnerabilities hoti hain. Inhe dementia, Alzheimer’s jaise health conditions se deal karna padta hai. NITI Aayog ke xx report ke hisaab se 75% se zyada buzurgon ko na koi chronic disease hai. Aur Max India ke data ke hisaab se 24% buzurg rozmarra ke kaam khud nahi kar paate hain. 
Limited financial resources aur badhte living aur medical expenses se bhi deal karna padta hai. Sath hi, Bharat mein social security provisions bhi insufficient hai. <Data/fact>. 

Dosto, pehle to usually joint families mein parivar ke log hi buzurgon ka khyal rakh lete the. Lekin ab Bharat ka family structure bhi badal raha hai. Max India ke investor presentation ke hisaab se, aaj 70% families nuclear hain. Bachche padhai aur naukri ke liye doosre shehron ya videsh chale jaate hain. Toh joint family wala jo default care system tha, voh tootta jaa raha hai. Aur iska asar akelepan me dikhta hai. HelpAge India ke 2025 ke survey me 10 shehron ke lagbhag 47% buzurgon ne loneliness ko ek serious problem bataya. 

Jis wajah se senior care ke need Bharat mein bhi ab badh rahi hai. Government estimates ke hisaab se, 2024 mein Bharat ki silver economy lagbhag 73,000 crores ki thi. Iss market ko hum do segments mein baat sakte hain. Aur 2030 tak ye market lagbhag 1 trillion rupees tak ka ho sakta hai. 

Ek market hai jaha need hai par accessibility nahi hai. 
Humaare desh mein ek taraf 78% buzurgon ke paas koi pension nahi hai. Aur sirf 18% ke paas health insurance hai. 

Doosri taraf aate hain affluent urban seniors. Jo retired hai, lekin financially independent hain. Jinke bachche bahar settled hain, aur jo 1-5 crore ka ghar khareed sakte hain. JLL ke hisaab se, overall senior population me 17 lakh seniors aise hain. Aur 2030 tak ye badh kar 21 lakh tak pohoch jaenge. Zyadatar companies abhi isi market ko target kar rahi hain. 

Yaani iss segment demand to badh raha hai. Aur aage aur bhi tezi se badhegi. 

Lekin Bharat mein abhi organised senior care living units kaafi kam hai. Aur jo existing facilities hain bhi wo 80-85% occupancy par operate kar rahi hain. JLL ke hisaab se aaj ke din Bharat mein sirf 25,050 organised senior living units hain. Yaani supply abhi addressable market ka sirf 1.5% cater kar pa rahi hai. Wahi America mein ye number 6-7% hai, aur New Zealand mein 14-15%. 

To isi demand supply gap ko dekhte huye kayi real estate companies, aur government, dono ne ab iss senior care segment pe focus karna shuru kar diya hai. JLL ke hisaab se, organised senior living supply annually 8.5% se grow kar rahi thi. Aur 2024 se June 2026 ke beech ye growth 14.2% per year ho gayi thi.
